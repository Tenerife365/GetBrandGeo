## Hard rules

- **No AI tells.** No em dashes or en dashes anywhere, ever. None of: delve,
  unlock, unleash, elevate, harness, leverage as a verb, game-changer,
  supercharge, revolutionize, seamless, robust, cutting-edge, transformative,
  "let's dive in", "it's not just X, it's Y", or a rhetorical-question opener.
  Write like a sharp operator writing to another operator.
- **Never invent a statistic, a customer, a logo or a testimonial.** BrandGEO
  sells measurement. A fabricated number destroys the premise. Every figure
  traces to a row in `ai_results`, a published research page in
  `brandgeo/web/`, or a cited external source with a URL.
- **Validate JSON-LD before anything ships.** Three FAQPage schemas were live
  and silently invalid for weeks on a product whose whole thesis is being parsed
  correctly by AI engines. Parse every block; do not eyeball it.
- **Two deploy pipelines, do not confuse them.** `getbrandgeo.com` deploys from
  `brandgeo/web/` via a GitHub webhook to cPanel. `app.getbrandgeo.com` deploys
  from `brandgeo-dashboard/` via Netlify. A push that touches only marketing is
  cancelled by Netlify on purpose.
- **Do not touch `brandgeo-dashboard/src/` or `netlify/functions/`.** Product
  code is another chat's scope. If content work needs a product change, write
  the ask down and hand it to me.
