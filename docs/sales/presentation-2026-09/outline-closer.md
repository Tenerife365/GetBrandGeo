# Closer outline: BrandGEO sales presentation

Lens: senior B2B SaaS closer. Built backward from the last slide. Every
slide's job is to earn the reader's attention for the next one, and the deck
ends at exactly one ask. Source of every claim: `fact-ledger.md` in this
folder, cited by ID. Reader profile, format and hard rules per
`.claude/handoffs/027-bg-orchestrator-to-deck-session-brandgeo-sales-presentation.md`
sections 3, 5 and 6.

Reader: a founder, marketing lead or agency owner at a small or mid size B2B
or local service company, zero GEO vocabulary, whose mental model is "I
searched my own brand in ChatGPT and I was there." Format: 16:9, 16 slides,
readable alone in five minutes, presentable live in fifteen.

The backward logic, stated once: the CTA (slide 16) only works if the reader
already knows what to do with the report (slide 15). That only lands if
pricing feels concrete, not abstract (slide 13 to 14). Pricing only feels
worth it if the two remaining objections are already dead (slides 11 and 5).
Both objections only die if the reader has already seen real proof (slides
6 to 10). Proof only means something once the reader can repeat back, in
their own words, what is being measured (slides 2 to 4). And none of it
lands if slide 1 does not first put them in the right frame: this is a
measurement, not a pitch.

## Appendix page template (one per industry)

Proposed for the separate appendix PDF, one page per vertical, laid out
identically so a page can be inserted or dropped per prospect without
touching the rest of the deck.

**Header band.** Industry name only, for example "AI Visibility for Real
Estate," same dark background and violet accent as the main deck (F31,
F33), small mono eyebrow line "BrandGEO industry brief."

**Headline stat block, 2 to 3 stats.** Pulled verbatim from that industry's
ledger entry (F62 to F71), each stat with its source name in small type
directly under the number, exactly as it appears in the ledger, no
rounding, no rephrasing of the figure itself. For example, for Law Firms
(F68): "77 to 78% of legal search queries now trigger a Google AI Overview,
per Semrush's 10-million-keyword analysis," "About 7 legal directories own
the AI citation layer for virtually every legal query category, per 5WPR
and Haute Lawyer Network, 2026."

**One buyer-question example.** A single illustrative, non-branded buyer
question in that vertical's own language, for example "best family law
attorney in [city]" for law firms, or "AI-friendly hotel near [landmark]"
for hotels. Illustrative only, not sourced to the ledger since it is a
product mechanism example, not a factual claim.

**What BrandGEO would show.** A small mock report row, same visual language
as slide 10 of the main deck (buyer question, engine icon, named or not
named, competitor if applicable), scaled down to one row so the page reads
in ten seconds.

**Footer.** Link to that industry's live research page URL (from F62 to
F71) in mono type, plus the standard small-print line: "No client names.
No invented numbers. Every figure sourced above." No CTA on appendix pages;
the free audit ask stays exclusive to slide 16 of the main deck.

Ten pages available today, one per industry page on the live site: e-commerce
(F62), education (F63), financial services (F64), healthcare (F65), home
services (F66), hotels (F67), law firms (F68), real estate (F69),
restaurants (F70), SaaS (F71). Append only the page or pages matching the
prospect's known industry; never all ten to a single prospect unless asked.

## Compliance self-check

- No em or en dashes anywhere in this file (verified by pattern scan before
  hand-off; re-scan required at build time against the rendered slide text
  too, since layout software can reintroduce them).
- No hype vocabulary: unlock, seamless, leverage, game-changing, landscape
  or relatives do not appear; "available from" replaces "unlocks" throughout.

  (slide 6) is referred to only as "the anonymised hero client," per F39.

  F37 ban.
- MCP access, Copilot and DeepSeek are not mentioned anywhere in this
  outline, per F14 and F91 (safer than the minimum bar of describing them as
  "not yet built," since the deck has no reason to raise them at all). Meta
  AI is not mentioned anywhere, per F05.
- Free audit CTA appears only on slide 16. The affiliate programme gets one
  line on slide 16, not a slide of its own, per the task brief.
- Contact email is constantin@getbrandgeo.com only, per F86 and the explicit
  task instruction, despite the live site itself showing a different address.
- At least three verticals appear in the core deck (home services, real
  estate and SaaS on slide 5); ten are available in the appendix.
- Pricing on slide 14 matches the live homepage's own five default-visible
  cards (F17 to F21) plus one summary line for the two cards hidden behind
  its mode switch (F22, F23), rather than inventing a shorter list.
