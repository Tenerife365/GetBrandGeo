# BrandGEO sales deck, outline (GEO and SEO practitioner lens)

Stage 1 output for `.claude/handoffs/027-bg-orchestrator-to-deck-session-brandgeo-sales-presentation.md`.
Lens: the reader already buys SEO. This outline's job is to show what GEO adds
on top of that spend, name the specific things BrandGEO measures that a rank
tracker cannot (engine by engine mention, position, sentiment, competitor
share), and use the research library as proof of method. Optimised for
credibility over urgency. Every claim below carries a fact ledger ID from
`docs/sales/presentation-2026-09/fact-ledger.md`; nothing here is sourced from
memory.

Format: 16:9, 16 slides. Reads alone in five minutes, presents live in
fifteen. No dashes, no hype vocabulary, no testimonials, no client names, no
73 percent or 4.2x, no MCP, no Copilot or DeepSeek as available, no Meta AI.
The free audit call to action appears once, on slide 16 only.

## Appendix page template, one per industry

The appendix is a separate document from the core deck (per the orchestrator
handoff section 2), appended only when a prospect's industry is known. Every
appendix page follows one fixed template so the set reads as one system:

**Header:** Small BrandGEO mark, top left. Industry name as the page title,
for example "AI Visibility in Real Estate." No page level call to action; the
appendix exists to extend the credibility case the core deck already made, not
to repeat the ask on slide 16.

**Take-away line:** One sentence, industry specific, in the same voice as the
core deck's slide titles. Example for real estate: "AI already sources higher
converting buyer leads than the largest listing portal." (F69.)

**Stat cards:** Two to three headline figures pulled directly from that
industry's page, each shown as a large number, one line of context, and the
source named in small mono type exactly as the fact ledger records it (for
example "SOCi, 2026 Local Visibility Index"). The ledger ID for each stat is
kept in the speaker notes for internal traceability, not printed on the slide.

**Tie back line:** One short sentence connecting the generic BrandGEO
capability to this vertical's buyer question, for example: "For a real estate
brand, that is the question 'best agent for first time buyers in [city],' not
your firm's name."

**Footer:** A thin violet accent bar, BrandGEO wordmark, page number, and the
source page URL from `getbrandgeo.com/ai-visibility-for-[industry].html` in
small mono type, matching the main deck's typography (Inter for body,
JetBrains Mono for data labels and URLs, per F34).

**Industry pages available for this template today (F57):** e-commerce,
education, financial services, healthcare, home services, hotels, law firms,
real estate, restaurants, SaaS. Slides 4, 10, and 11 of the core deck already
draw from SaaS, real estate, home services, and law firms; the appendix can
reuse those or pull fresh stats from the same pages without repeating a
figure already shown in the core deck.
