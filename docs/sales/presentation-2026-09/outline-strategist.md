# Outline: SaaS Positioning Strategist lens

Stage 1 output for `.claude/handoffs/027-bg-orchestrator-to-deck-session-brandgeo-sales-presentation.md`.
Author lens: category shift (search to answers), cost of absence, why a
measurement product is the right entry point. Every claim cites a fact
ledger ID from `docs/sales/presentation-2026-09/fact-ledger.md`. No em or en
dashes appear anywhere in this file; ranges use "to" and separations use
commas.

Thesis in one line: the reader already tested the wrong question by
searching their own brand name, so slides 1 to 3 replace that test with the
real one (a buyer's category question, across five engines, scored honestly),
slides 4 to 11 prove the gap is real, growing and revenue bearing with
published research behind it, and slides 12 to 15 turn that proof into a
plan, a price and one free next step.

15 slides. Reading time under five minutes, live walkthrough under fifteen.

## Appendix page template (one per industry, proposed)

A separate PDF, one slide per vertical, appended only when the prospect's
industry is known (per the handoff section 2 deliverable table). Every
appendix page uses this identical layout so the set reads as one system:

1. **Header row:** industry name (for example "Real estate agencies") in
   Instrument Serif, plus a single small icon, top left. `getbrandgeo.com`
   page URL for that vertical in mono type, top right, sourced from F57 and
   section 6.3 of the fact ledger (F62 to F71).
2. **Headline stat:** the single most striking sourced figure for that
   vertical, set very large, center, in the same style as slide 3 and slide
   11 (Instrument Serif number, mono source line directly beneath it, one
   sentence, always cites the ledger ID and the original source name from
   the fact ledger, for example "per FlyDragon, 2026 (F69)").
3. **Two supporting stats:** smaller stat chips, left and right of center,
   each with its own one-line source citation. Pulled from the same
   industry's ledger entry so all three numbers on the page come from one
   consistent block (F62 to F71), never mixed across verticals.
4. **One relevance line:** a single sentence, no more than 20 words,
   translating the stat into what it means for that reader's revenue, for
   example "If nine in ten of your competitors are invisible here too, the
   gap is closable, not just real." Written fresh per vertical, never a
   template placeholder left unfilled.
5. **Footer:** BrandGEO mark, small, bottom right (F72, F73 depending on
   the appendix's background theme). No CTA, no pricing, no contact
   information repeats here, since the core deck's slide 15 already carries
   the single CTA and the appendix is inserted behind it, never in front.
