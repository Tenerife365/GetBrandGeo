## 3. Blind spots, ranked

Ranked by how much each one prevents the sprint from learning, not by how hard
it is to fix.

### B1. Nobody knows how many people visit the site. Severity: highest.

What it prevents: **the entire denominator.** Without a visitor count, "2
external audits ever" cannot be read. Two audits from 20 visitors is a 10
percent conversion rate and a distribution problem. Two audits from 5,000
visitors is a landing page problem. These call for opposite work, and the sprint
will pick one blind. Every channel this sprint fires (directories, social,
outbound, Search Console submissions) is judged on traffic, so with no traffic
number, G3 ("three channels firing daily") can be verified as *fired* by
`gtm-verify` but never as *working*.

Why: Plausible removed 2026-08-07 (`1b9bd24`), off by ruling and staying off.
GA4 is consent-gated. So for 2026-08-07 onward there is no instrument any agent
can read.

Cheapest fix: **the cPanel raw access logs, and they are free.** See B2.

### B2. GA4 undercounts by an unknown factor. Severity: high.

What it prevents: GA4 will produce a session number during this sprint, and it
will be wrong low by an amount nobody can state. A seat that quotes it without
the qualifier will make a false claim. Worse, the undercount is not a constant:
consent rates vary by traffic source, so a directory audience and an outbound
audience can produce different GA4 coverage for identical real traffic, which
makes cross-channel comparison inside GA4 unsafe.

MEASURED mechanism: `brandgeo/web/ga4-init.js` blocks the googletagmanager.com
request entirely until the visitor clicks accept. This is deliberate and correct
for a Spanish establishment under AEPD guidance, and it is documented at length
in the file header. It is not a defect and must not be "fixed" by removing the
gate.

**Can cPanel raw access logs close the consent gap? Yes, and this is the finding
that matters most in this section.** Raw access logs are written by the web
server before any JavaScript runs, so they are entirely consent-independent and
count every request including from visitors who declined. In the Apache combined
log format they record the full request line, which means **the query string is
in there, so `?utm_source=producthunt` is captured in the logs even though no
database column holds it** (INFERRED from the standard combined format; the
exact `LogFormat` on 91.200.121.45 is unverified and Constantin should confirm
it when he pulls the first file). They also record the `Referer` header, so
"came from producthunt.com" is recoverable without any code change at all.

What they cannot do: separate humans from bots without filtering (expect a large
share of the raw hits to be crawlers), stitch sessions reliably, or say anything
about what happened on the page. They are a volume and a source instrument, not
a behaviour instrument.

Cheapest fix, in order:
1. **Constantin pulls one log file. EUR 0, about 3 minutes.** cPanel >
   **Metrics** > **Raw Access** > download the archived `.gz` for
   getbrandgeo.com and drop it in the repo working tree (git-ignored). I parse
   it and the sprint has a real visitor count, a referrer breakdown, and UTM
   attribution retroactively, for the whole period since 08-07.
2. Failing that, cPanel > **Metrics** > **Awstats** > getbrandgeo.com gives a
   rendered monthly view with visitors, hits and top referrers. Screenshot-only,
   which means I cannot compute deltas from it, but it is one click.

### B3. No signup or lead row records where the person came from. Severity: high.

See section 5. This is the difference between "the sprint produced 14 signups"
and "Product Hunt produced 9 of the 14 signups", which is the only question that
decides what the next sprint does.

### B4. Google does not know most of the site exists. Severity: medium for this sprint, high beyond it.

What it prevents: knowing whether the roughly 100 live pages are an asset or
just files on a disk. Sitemap pings go to IndexNow only, so Google gets nothing
automatic (`job_runs` detail carries `google_skipped: NO_CREDENTIALS` by ruling).
Only Search Console can answer the coverage question, and only Constantin can
open it.

Cheapest fix: C4 above, one screen, about 1 minute, and it is a prerequisite for
`gtm-content` prioritising which 20 URLs to submit by hand.

### B5. The public audit scores zero, every time, 8 for 8. Severity: unknown, potentially fatal to the whole sprint.

Recorded in section 2a. This is not a measurement blind spot exactly, it is a
measured anomaly whose meaning is a blind spot: I cannot tell from the data
whether the public path is broken or whether those specific domains genuinely
score zero. It sits here because if it is a defect, every number this sprint
tries to move is capped at zero regardless of channel work.

Cheapest fix: one live run of the public audit against a domain known to have
strong AI presence, by anyone, then compare to an internal audit of the same
domain. Under 5 minutes. Owner: `gtm-conversion` to brief, `bg-verify` to check.

### B6. Nothing brings a user back and nothing records that they left. Severity: medium.

No lifecycle email exists, no error monitor, no exit survey, no churn record.
So the sprint cannot measure why anyone stopped, only that they did. Named here
per the charter rather than fixed: these are build items in the plan's section 5
queue, not measurement items.

Instrumentation that would fix it and its cost: a "your refresh ran, here is
what changed" email is already the first item of the plan's build queue, and it
is the only comeback mechanism that would exist. Cost is a `bg-backend` build
plus one Netlify deploy from a budget of roughly two a day.

## 5. Attribution

**Today it cannot be done. There is no mechanism, anywhere, to distinguish a
signup that arrived from Product Hunt from one that arrived organically.**
MEASURED, three ways, on 2026-08-13:

1. `prospect_audits` has 24 columns and none of them is a referrer, a UTM, or a
   channel. Full column list [Q13].
2. `prospect_leads` has a `source` column and it holds the literal string
   `instant_audit` on both of its 2 rows [Q14]. That records the mechanism, not
   the channel, and `docs/growth/channel-attribution-spec.md` line 14 explicitly
   warns against overloading it.
3. `grep -rn "utm_" brandgeo/web/*.js brandgeo/web/index.html
   brandgeo-dashboard/src brandgeo-dashboard/netlify/functions` returns **zero
   matches**, and `grep -rn "document.referrer"` across the same paths returns
   **zero matches**. Nothing reads a UTM and nothing reads a referrer, anywhere
   in the product.

`terms_acceptances` does carry `origin`, but all 12 rows read
`https://getbrandgeo.com` [Q7], which is the site the form was posted from, not
where the visitor came from. It is not an attribution field.

So the honest answer for Day 1 is: **if a signup arrives tomorrow from Product
Hunt, the database will not know, and neither will I.** GA4 would know, for the
subset of visitors who accepted consent, and only at session level: it can say
"a Product Hunt session happened" but it cannot join that session to the
`auth.users` row that appeared 20 minutes later.

### The UTM convention, binding on every gtm- seat from now on

Any URL any seat publishes anywhere that points at getbrandgeo.com carries all
three parameters. No exceptions, including in outbound email bodies and
directory listing fields.

```
https://getbrandgeo.com/?utm_source=<platform>&utm_medium=<type>&utm_campaign=sprint17
```

- `utm_source` is the **platform**, lowercase, no spaces, hyphens only:
  `producthunt`, `g2`, `alternativeto`, `saashub`, `indiehackers`, `uneed`,
  `fazier`, `devhunt`, `linkedin`, `x`, `threads`, `facebook`, `instagram`,
  `tiktok`, `youtube`, `gbp`, `coldemail`, `newsletter`.
- `utm_medium` is the **placement type**, from this closed set only:
  `listing` (a directory entry), `social` (an organic post), `bio` (a profile
  link), `email` (cold outbound), `article` (a link inside published content),
  `comment` (a reply or maker comment), `profile` (a company page field).
- `utm_campaign` is `sprint17` for everything fired between 2026-08-13 and
  2026-08-29. It is what lets Day 17 separate sprint traffic from everything
  before it. Do not invent per-post campaign values; that granularity is not
  readable with the instruments available.
- Values are lowercase, `[a-z0-9-]` only, under 40 characters. They end up in a
  URL a stranger can rewrite, and later in a database column.
- **Deep links carry the same tags.** A directory pointing at
  `/brandgeo-vs-peec.html` still needs all three.
- Where a platform allows exactly one link for the whole sprint (Instagram,
  TikTok, Threads bios), that one link gets `utm_medium=bio` and the sprint gets
  platform-level attribution and nothing finer. That limitation is structural,
  per `channel-attribution-spec.md`, and is accepted rather than worked around.

### What the convention buys, and what it does not

With tagging alone and no code change, and **only for consent-accepting
visitors**, GA4 reports the split under Traffic acquisition >
`Session source / medium`. That answers "which platform sent sessions". It does
not answer "which platform sent the customer".

To answer the customer question, `docs/growth/channel-attribution-spec.md`
already specifies the fix in full and it does not need rewriting: four nullable
columns on `prospect_leads` (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`,
`landing_referrer`), `sessionStorage` persistence in `brandgeo/web/site.js` so
the tag survives the hop from an article page to the homepage, and pass-through
in `unlock-audit-report.js`. Missing values must be `null`, never `'direct'` or
`'unknown'`.

**That spec requires a schema change, so this seat writes the requirement and
routes it rather than applying it.** Route: `bg-architect` for the contract,
then `bg-backend`, briefed through `gtm-conversion`, batched into the same
Netlify deploy as the painkiller unlock so it costs one build and not three.
Deploy order is load-bearing and is in the spec: migration first, function
second, `site.js` last.

Until that ships, **the sprint's attribution is: UTM tags in every link, GA4
source/medium for the consented subset, and the cPanel access log as the
consent-independent cross-check.** Anything finer than that would be inference
presented as record, and this seat does not write those down.

## Appendix: the queries

Supabase project `duiyifepitvugyulobqm`, `SELECT` only, run 2026-08-13 14:29 UTC.
No `INSERT`, `UPDATE`, `DELETE`, `ALTER`, `CREATE` or write RPC was issued in
this session.

Standing exclusion predicates, used throughout:

- Internal or research clients: `c.category <> 'research' AND c.id NOT IN (1, 2, 19, 51)`
- Internal audit batches: `created_via = 'public'`
- Founder-owned audit domains: `domain NOT IN ('getbrandgeo.com', 'talentwelove.com')`

**[Q1] Audits, public and external.**
```sql
SELECT to_char(created_at,'YYYY-MM-DD HH24:MI') AS created_at_utc, domain,
       created_via, status, unlocked,
       (email_captured_at IS NOT NULL) AS email_captured, ai_score
FROM prospect_audits
WHERE created_via = 'public'
ORDER BY created_at;
```

**[Q2] Leads.**
```sql
SELECT count(*) AS leads_all_time,
       count(*) FILTER (WHERE created_at >= now() - interval '7 days') AS leads_7d,
       count(*) FILTER (WHERE hubspot_error IS NOT NULL) AS with_hubspot_error,
       count(*) FILTER (WHERE hubspot_synced) AS synced
FROM prospect_leads;
```

**[Q3] Users.**
```sql
SELECT count(*) AS users_all_time,
       count(*) FILTER (WHERE created_at >= now() - interval '7 days') AS users_7d,
       count(*) FILTER (WHERE last_sign_in_at >= now() - interval '7 days') AS signed_in_7d,
       max(created_at) AS last_signup_at,
       max(last_sign_in_at) AS last_sign_in_at
FROM auth.users;
```

**[Q4] Clients by category and plan, and the customer-side count.**
```sql
SELECT category, plan, count(*) AS n FROM clients GROUP BY 1,2 ORDER BY 1,2;

SELECT count(*) AS customer_side_clients
FROM clients c
WHERE c.category <> 'research' AND c.id NOT IN (1, 2, 19, 51);
```

**[Q5] Activated free accounts, the G2 headline metric.**
```sql
SELECT count(*) AS activated_free_accounts
FROM clients c
WHERE c.category <> 'research'
  AND c.id NOT IN (1, 2, 19, 51)
  AND c.plan = 'free'
  AND EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM ai_results r
              WHERE r.client_id = c.id AND r.status = 'ok');
```

**[Q6] Collection runs, and who triggered them.**
```sql
SELECT r.trigger,
       count(*) AS runs,
       count(*) FILTER (WHERE p.role = 'viewer') AS by_viewer,
       count(*) FILTER (WHERE p.role = 'admin')  AS by_admin,
       count(*) FILTER (WHERE r.created_by IS NULL) AS no_actor,
       count(*) FILTER (WHERE p.role = 'viewer'
                        AND c.category <> 'research'
                        AND c.id NOT IN (1, 2, 19, 51)) AS customer_triggered
FROM collection_runs r
LEFT JOIN user_profiles p ON p.id = r.created_by
LEFT JOIN clients c ON c.id = r.client_id
GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 2 DESC;
```

**[Q7] Checkout intent and completion.**
```sql
SELECT (SELECT count(*) FROM terms_acceptances) AS intents_all_time,
       (SELECT count(*) FROM terms_acceptances
         WHERE created_at >= now() - interval '7 days') AS intents_7d,
       (SELECT count(*) FROM stripe_events
         WHERE type = 'checkout.session.completed') AS checkouts_all_time;

SELECT origin, count(*) FROM terms_acceptances GROUP BY 1;
```

**[Q8] Result volume, and who produced it.**
```sql
SELECT r.client_id, c.name, c.category, count(*) AS rows_7d,
       min(COALESCE(r.checked_at, r.created_at))::date AS first_day,
       max(COALESCE(r.checked_at, r.created_at))::date AS last_day
FROM ai_results r LEFT JOIN clients c ON c.id = r.client_id
WHERE COALESCE(r.checked_at, r.created_at) >= now() - interval '7 days'
GROUP BY 1,2,3 ORDER BY 4 DESC;
```

**[Q9] Refresh cadence distribution.**
```sql
SELECT refresh_cadence, count(*) AS n,
       count(*) FILTER (WHERE category = 'research')  AS research,
       count(*) FILTER (WHERE category <> 'research') AS non_research
FROM clients GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 2 DESC;
```

**[Q10] Job health.**
```sql
SELECT job, count(*) AS runs, count(*) FILTER (WHERE ok) AS ok_runs,
       max(ran_at) AS last_run,
       (SELECT count(*) FROM sitemap_pings) AS sitemap_pings_rows,
       (SELECT max(last_pinged_at) FROM sitemap_pings) AS sitemap_last_ping
FROM job_runs GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 1;
```

**[Q11] Audit score distribution, public against internal.**
```sql
SELECT created_via, count(*) AS n,
       count(*) FILTER (WHERE ai_score = 0) AS score_zero,
       count(*) FILTER (WHERE ai_score > 0) AS score_gt_zero,
       min(ai_score) AS min_score, max(ai_score) AS max_score
FROM prospect_audits GROUP BY 1;
```

**[Q12] THE DAILY QUERY. One statement, every pulled scoreboard cell.**
Replace `DATE '2026-08-14'` with the day being closed.
```sql
WITH d AS (SELECT DATE '2026-08-14' AS day)
SELECT
  (SELECT day FROM d) AS scoreboard_date,
  (SELECT count(*) FROM prospect_audits
     WHERE created_via = 'public'
       AND domain NOT IN ('getbrandgeo.com','talentwelove.com')) AS p1_external_audits_all_time,
  (SELECT count(*) FROM prospect_audits, d
     WHERE created_via = 'public'
       AND domain NOT IN ('getbrandgeo.com','talentwelove.com')
       AND created_at::date = d.day) AS p2_external_audits_today,
  (SELECT count(*) FROM prospect_audits
     WHERE created_via = 'public') AS p3_public_audits_all_time,
  (SELECT count(*) FROM prospect_audits
     WHERE created_via = 'public' AND unlocked
       AND domain NOT IN ('getbrandgeo.com','talentwelove.com')) AS p4_external_unlocks_all_time,
  (SELECT count(*) FROM prospect_leads) AS p5_leads_all_time,
  (SELECT count(*) FROM auth.users, d
     WHERE created_at::date = d.day) AS p6_new_users_today,
  (SELECT count(*) FROM auth.users) AS p7_users_all_time,
  (SELECT count(*) FROM clients c
     WHERE c.category <> 'research'
       AND c.id NOT IN (1,2,19,51)) AS p8_customer_clients_all_time,
  (SELECT count(*) FROM clients c
     WHERE c.category <> 'research' AND c.id NOT IN (1,2,19,51)
       AND c.plan = 'free'
       AND EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM ai_results r
                   WHERE r.client_id = c.id AND r.status='ok')) AS p9_activated_free_accounts,
  (SELECT count(*) FROM collection_runs r
     JOIN user_profiles p ON p.id = r.created_by
     JOIN clients c ON c.id = r.client_id
     WHERE p.role = 'viewer' AND c.category <> 'research'
       AND c.id NOT IN (1,2,19,51)) AS p10_customer_triggered_runs_all_time,

     WHERE type = 'checkout.session.completed') AS p12_checkouts_all_time,
  (SELECT count(*) FROM ai_results
     WHERE COALESCE(checked_at, created_at) >= now() - interval '7 days') AS p13_ai_results_7d,
```
