# 07. YouTube

Four Shorts and four long-form pieces, one of each per pillar. Nothing here is
scheduled or posted. Drafts for review.

Every Short is a standalone argument, not a trailer for the long-form on the
same pillar. A viewer who watches only the Short should leave with a complete
idea. A viewer who watches only the long-form should not feel they missed a
setup.

**Sourcing.** Every number below traces to the pillar's own research page or to
`planConfig.ts` as pinned in `00-BRIEF.md`. No Grok rate and no AI Overviews
rate appears anywhere in this file, in copy or on screen, for the reason the
brief gives: one collection day is not a measurement, and publishing a
percentage off it from a company that sells measurement would refute the
product.

**Meta AI** appears six times, and every one of them carries the 16 July 2026
retirement in the same sentence or the one beside it. Two are statements of the
removal itself, in the P1 assets. Four are dated historical records of what it
answered in July 2026, in the P2 and P4 assets, where cutting it would falsify
the research rather than tidy it. It is never listed as a live engine anywhere.

**Title:** `Why our scorer returns null instead of a rank`

**Pinned comment:**
> The three signals, the 25 phrases, the 17 overrides, and the fallback we
> removed: https://getbrandgeo.com/bg-019.html

**Description:**
```
BrandGEO only reports a numeric rank when an AI engine's answer makes one of
three explicit, checkable claims: a genuine numbered list, a bulleted list whose
lead-in states an order, or a superlative grammatically attached to the brand.
Everything else returns null, including a sentence position that looks like a
plausible rank.

Three details from the write-up:

- A numbered-list digit is only trusted between 1 and 50. A real line in our test
  data starts "2019." and without that band the brand scores rank 2019.
- Bullets need one of 25 ordering phrases before position counts at all, and 17
  counter-phrases like "a few" or "no particular order" override it even when an
  ordering word is present.
- We removed the sentence-index fallback rather than tightening it. "Third
  sentence to mention the brand" and "third-ranked recommendation" are not the
  same unit.

A tool that never returns null for a mentioned brand has not solved the problem.
It has moved the guessing somewhere you cannot see.

Full methodology: https://getbrandgeo.com/bg-019.html

```
