# BrandGEO, long-form brand introduction

**Runtime target: 6 minutes 20 seconds.** Fully produced, no camera. Voiceover
over screen recordings, motion cards and data visualisations.

**Hook driver: #5, concrete proof** (skill section 3). "Here is the exact result
and the exact date." A curiosity turn carries scenes 2 and 3, but the spine is
proof, and that is deliberate: this is the first long-form asset on a channel
that sells measurement, so the opening has to be a measurement.

**Why not the other five drivers.** Status threat and loss aversion each already
run twice in the nine published Shorts, and both, applied to a brand
introduction, make a claim about this viewer's own visibility that nobody has
measured. That fault was caught and corrected in the Shorts titles already
(`../shorts/POSTS.md`, changes 1 and 3). Contrarian ("ranking first in Google
does not mean you exist in an AI answer") is true and is used in Short 4, but a
competitor could publish that sentence tonight with a find-and-replace on the
brand name, so it fails the skill's own specificity test as a channel-defining
open. Utility is the close of this video, not its spine.

**Funnel stage: MOFU.** Goal is belief that the problem is real and measurable.
Primary CTA is the free audit. Metric is watch time and audit starts.

---

## Runtime judgement, stated because the format decision invites it

**Six minutes twenty, not ten.** With no talking head every second is an asset
somebody has to build, and the honest inventory below supports about six minutes
of real product and real measured results. Ten minutes would be four minutes of
motion graphics restating claims already made. On a channel whose product is
measurement, padding is the wrong signal, and it is also the expensive kind of
wrong: each padded minute is roughly two more capture sessions from Constantin.

If a cut is needed, remove section 6 (Fairness) last, not first. It is the
section that does the most differentiating work, and it is also the cheapest to
shoot because it is entirely motion cards.

## Compliance notes on this script

Checked by reading, not by a script. A scanner is listed as owed in
`OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` because per the shared brief section 4 an unverified scanner
reports clean because it cannot see, and building one here without injecting its
own defects would be worse than declaring the gap.

- **Dashes.** No em dash, no en dash, no minus used as punctuation, anywhere in
  this file including headings.
- **Banned vocabulary.** None of the listed terms appears.
- **Rhetorical question opener.** None. There is no question mark in the
  narration at all.
- **Superlatives about the research program.** None. The Boston page's own "most
  5/5-dense city in this program" and the Chicago page's "first fully unanimous
  result" are both deliberately unused, because they contradict each other and a
  page asserting a program-wide maximum cannot be its own source for it.
- **Universals.** "Nobody controls that" at 5:14 was checked against the shared
  brief's refinement. It quantifies over people, so under the strict reading it
  is a universal. It is kept because it is a claim about a mechanism rather than
  a slight on anyone, and because the sentence it sits in is a concession against
  our own interest. **Flagged for a human ruling in `OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` rather
  than silently kept.** A safe rewrite is supplied there.
- **Named measured subjects.** None. Every firm, city-named company and person
  from the source pages is withheld. The cities themselves are also unnamed in
  the narration, which goes further than the rule requires and costs nothing.
- **Engine names.** ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google
  AI Overviews and Grok appear as the instruments being measured, which the
  shared brief explicitly permits.
- **Historical versus current lineup.** Never mixed in one sentence. The 5-engine
  runs are stated as five, dated, and the 4-engine Paris run carries its own
  correction card at 3:28. The current lineup of seven is stated only in section
  3, with its own date.
- **Meta AI.** Never listed as live. Referred to at 3:28 only as an engine since
  retired, which is what it is.
- **Pricing.** One factual line at 6:06, at the close of a MOFU asset. Flagged in
  `OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` as a judgement call, since the skill's rule is written for
  TOFU.
- **Invented customer proof.** None. There is no testimonial, no logo wall, no
  case study and no customer count anywhere in this script.
