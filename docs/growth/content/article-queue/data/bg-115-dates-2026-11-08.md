# BG-115 data record: years and "as of" in AI answers (2026-11-08)

Topic 13 `dates` from `topics.md`. Read only; SELECT and WITH only. No person's name, address
or phone number is recorded here: the repository is public.

## Basis

- Clients 28 to 47 (20 US city research clients), `ai_results.status = 'ok'`,
  `checked_at < '2026-11-01'` (July collection only; Los Angeles November rows left out).
- 585 answers: chatgpt 114, claude 120, gemini 120, google_ai 111, perplexity 120. The brief's
  591 is now 585 because the 6 July ChatGPT rows of client 28 were deleted on 2026-11-06.
- Collected 2026-07-24 and 2026-07-25. So the current year at collection was 2026.
- Count source: `response_text`, links removed with
  `regexp_replace(t,'(https?://|www\.)\S+',' ','g')`, backslashes stripped, so years inside URLs
  do not count.
- Stored text is capped (chatgpt and google_ai at 10,000 characters, claude at about 3,800), so
  "anywhere in the answer" counts are lower bounds for those engines. The opening counts use the
  first 400 characters only, which no cap reaches.

## Patterns

- A year: `\m20(1\d|2[0-6])\M`. Per year: `\m2026\M`, `\m2025\M`, `\m2024\M`, `\m20(1\d|2[0-3])\M`.
- "as of": `\mas of\M`, case insensitive.
- Year framing in the opening (first 400 characters, en dash replaced by a hyphen):
  `(?:\m(?:for|in)\s+|\()(20[12]\d(?:\s*-\s*(?:20)?\d\d)?)(?:\s*:|\))`, i.e. "for 2025:",
  "in 2025-2026:", "(2025)". Every match was read by hand; see below.
- Out of date warning: `out of date|outdated|may have changed|may not be current|check (for )?(the )?(latest|current)|information (may|might) (be|have)`.

## Hand read

- Every one of the 23 opening framing matches (22 Claude, 1 Google AI Mode) was read. 21 are the
  answer framing itself by year (a heading or lead sentence such as "top-rated property
  management companies in <city> for 2025:"). 2 Claude matches are not: one cites a hospital
  ranking's edition "(2025-2026)", one an award edition "(2026)". Both removed, leaving 20 for
  Claude.
- All 37 non-Claude answers with a year in the first 400 characters were read and classed by
  hand: framing the answer by year (the answer says it is "for 2026", "as of 2026", "based on
  2026 data") or a year belonging to an award, ranking edition or source page.
- 20 more Claude openings with "2025" were read (random order by md5 of id): the year is either
  the framing above or an award year, never a founding year in the first 400 characters.
- The 2 "out of date" matches: one ChatGPT line telling the reader to check current reviews of
  one firm, one Perplexity refusal saying it cannot name agents without risking outdated
  recommendations (a BG-107 row). Neither warns that the answer itself may be out of date.

## Queries

```sql
with p as (select id, client_id, row_number() over (partition by client_id order by id) rn
           from prompts where client_id between 28 and 47),
r as (select a.id, a.client_id, a.llm, p.rn,
        replace(regexp_replace(a.response_text,'(https?://|www\.)\S+',' ','g'), chr(92), '') t
      from ai_results a join p on p.id = a.prompt_id
      where a.client_id between 28 and 47 and a.status = 'ok' and a.checked_at < '2026-11-01'),
f as (select *, (t ~ '\m20(1\d|2[0-6])\M') anyyear,
  (left(t,400) ~ '\m20(1\d|2[0-6])\M') early,
  (t ~ '\m2026\M') y26, (t ~ '\m2025\M') y25, (t ~ '\m2024\M') y24,
  (t ~ '\m20(1\d|2[0-3])\M') older, (t ~* '\mas of\M') asof
  from r)
select coalesce(llm,'all') llm, count(*) n, sum(anyyear::int) anyyear, sum(early::int) early,
 sum(y26::int) y26, sum(y25::int) y25, sum(y24::int) y24, sum(older::int) older, sum(asof::int) asof
from f group by rollup(llm) order by llm;
```

Opening framing, listed for the hand read:

```sql
with p as (select id, client_id, row_number() over (partition by client_id order by id) rn
           from prompts where client_id between 28 and 47),
r as (select a.id, a.llm, p.rn,
        replace(left(replace(regexp_replace(a.response_text,'(https?://|www\.)\S+',' ','g'), chr(92), ''),400), chr(8211), '-') t0
      from ai_results a join p on p.id = a.prompt_id
      where a.client_id between 28 and 47 and a.status = 'ok' and a.checked_at < '2026-11-01'),
m as (select id, llm, rn,
        substring(t0 from '(?:\m(?:for|in)\s+|\()(20[12]\d(?:\s*-\s*(?:20)?\d\d)?)(?:\s*:|\))') yr
      from r)
select id, llm, rn, yr from m where yr is not null order by llm, yr;
```

Non-Claude openings for the hand read: the same `r` with `a.llm <> 'claude'` and
`where t0 ~ '\m20[12]\d\M'`.
