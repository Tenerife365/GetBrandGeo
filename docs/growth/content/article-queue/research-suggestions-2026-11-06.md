## Briefs

### 13 Nov: ChatGPT search at a fifth of Google
- What happened: on 3 November 2026 OpenAI's ChatGPT consumer product lead said at an
  OpenAI dinner in New York that ChatGPT's volume of "search-like queries is now roughly
  one-fifth the volume of queries of Google"; OpenAI also put weekly users at 1.2 billion,
  up from 1 billion in about 60 days. Grade: reported (Big Technology, 3 Nov 2026,
  https://www.bigtechnology.com/p/openai-chatgpt-search-is-about-20).
- The caveat is the article: "search-like" was not defined, and the figure is OpenAI's own,
  with no method, period or independent check.
- BrandGEO angle: put it next to BrightLocal's March 2026 survey (31 percent of US
  consumers used ChatGPT for a business recommendation in the last 12 months, primary,
  https://www.brightlocal.com/research/lcrs-ai-trust/) and to what our studies show
  ChatGPT does with a local question (BG-108 ratings, BG-109 address and phone, BG-111
  verify before hiring).

### 15 Nov: Search Console and the UK deadline of 3 December
- What happened: the UK Competition and Markets Authority requires Google to give
  publishers clear engagement metrics for its AI search features (impressions, clicks and
  click-through rate across AI Overviews and AI Mode, separated from organic results) by
  3 December; page-level controls follow three months later; a first compliance report is
  due 3 January 2027. Google's search team said it has "nothing to announce yet".
  Grade: reported (Search Engine Journal, 2 Nov 2026,
  https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-ai-click-reporting-uk-deadline/595663/).
- Today: the generative AI performance report (announced 3 June 2026, all sites by 31
  August) shows impressions only, for AI Overviews, AI Mode and Discover; clicks sit inside
  the normal Performance report and cannot be separated. Grade: primary
  (https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports).
- Angle: a how-to for reading the report now, and what to look for after 3 December.
  Publish before the deadline; a short follow-up after it is a natural December slot.

### 18 Nov: gaming AI recommendations, the SafeGEO paper
- What happened: SafeGEO, EMNLP 2026 (October 2026), built 22 kinds of GEO attack across
  600 recommendation cases. Attacks raised the chance that a flawed product entered an AI
  agent's recommendation set by up to 83.2 percentage points; simple defences cut harmful
  promotion by up to 39.2 points but did not restore the baseline. Grade: primary
  (https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.603/).
- Angle: the difference between making true facts easy to find and manipulating an agent,
  and why the second is a risk to the business that does it. Products in the paper, not
  local firms; say so.

### 19 Nov: 45 percent ask AI, almost all double check
- What happened: BrightLocal's Local Consumer Review Survey 2026 (10 March 2026, 1,002 US
  adults): 45 percent used AI tools for local business recommendations, up from 6 percent;
  97 percent of AI users sometimes double check against real reviews; 42 percent always
  check reviews on review platforms; ChatGPT 31 percent, Google AI Mode 23 percent.
  Grade: primary (https://www.brightlocal.com/research/lcrs-ai-trust/).
- Not new news (March), but the numbers carry the month's theme and pair with our own
  finding that engines tell buyers to verify (BG-111). A separate "75 percent check Google
  or Maps" figure from a July survey is unverified; leave it out unless read at source.

### 21 Nov: SOCi's index next to our city data
