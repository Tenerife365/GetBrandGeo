# SPRINT-100: the 30-day baseline plan (adaptive)

Window: 2026-08-01 (Day 1) to 2026-08-30 (Day 30). Target: 100 paying
subscribers. Registry and kickoffs: `SPRINT-100-KICKOFFS-2026-07-31.md`.
Scoreboard: `SPRINT-100-SCOREBOARD.md`. Daily briefs: `SPRINT-100-BRIEFS.md`.

**This file is a baseline, not a contract.** The daily council cycle
(`SPRINT-100-DAILY-COUNCIL.md`, runs 03:00 local) adapts it every night
against the scoreboard. Rules of adaptation:

1. Past days are history. Never rewrite a past day's row; what happened is
   recorded in the scoreboard and briefs.
2. Today and future days may be edited freely, but every edit appends an
   entry to the Revision log below: date, trigger (what the measurement
   showed), the change, and which council seat's rule drove it.
3. The gates and the pre-agreed responses (Part A and Part C of the
   registry) are binding. The council applies them; it does not re-debate
   them at 3 AM. Changing a gate itself is Constantin's decision, queued to
   the morning brief.
4. Glidepath column is cumulative paying subscribers expected by end of that
   day. Red = two consecutive days more than 20 percent under glidepath;
   red triggers the relevant pre-agreed response, not improvisation.

## Standing daily blocks (Path 1 cadence)

Morning: send block (cold email batch fires, DMs by hand). Midday: replies
within 2 hours, calls. Afternoon: onboarding, agent supervision, one
evidence post. Evening: tell the open council chat to **close the day**; the
chat pulls Stripe and Supabase numbers, asks one line for sends and replies,
writes the scoreboard row and commits. Constantin edits no files. Weekends:
reduced sends (deliverability and reply rates drop), content and prep
instead.

## Notes the daily council must respect

- Send volumes assume S8 warmup matured on schedule; if deliverability is
  not proven (mail-tester 9+) by Day 6, cold volume waits and DM volume
  rises to compensate. Never burn the domain to keep a number.
- Founding offer sends are Constantin personal, not the cold tool.
- Day 15 fallback to Path 2 means: sends drop to 40/day, DMs to 15, Sundays
  off, and the expected landing is re-baselined in the revision log. The
  target stays 100; the claim of reaching it changes honestly.
- Nothing in this table authorizes spending (collection runs), sending by
  agents, or billing writes at night. Day actions belong to Constantin and
  the day-window chats.
