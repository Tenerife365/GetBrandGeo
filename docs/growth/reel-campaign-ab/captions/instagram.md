# Instagram captions, reel campaign A/B

Nine cuts, one per run folder. Everything below is ready to paste with no edit.

## How to use this

**Upload the `-silent.mp4` file, not the `-scored.mp4` file.** The silent master
carries no audio stream at all. Add music inside Instagram from its own audio
library at the point of posting. In app audio is licensed for the platform and
is favoured by its distribution, so a silent master plus a library track reaches
further than the same video with our own score baked in.

The `-scored.mp4` in the same folder is the paid and embed copy. Use it for ad
accounts, for the site, and for decks, where the in app library does not exist.

The `-cover.png` in each folder is a genuine frame 0 of the master, so the cover
already matches the opening frame. There is no need to pick a thumbnail.

Order to post: the nine cuts run three hook families twice over, plus contrarian,
concrete proof and utility once each. Spread them rather than posting the two
passes of the same driver close together, or the A/B tells you nothing.

Each block below gives the exact upload path, what the cut argues, then four
copy blocks. Caption, hashtags, first comment, alt text. The hashtags are a
separate block on purpose so you can drop them at the end of the caption or into
the first comment, whichever you are testing.

**Every caption extends the video rather than restating it.** The on screen text
is the argument. The caption is the mechanism, the limit, or the practical read
that would not fit in a card. Do not summarise the video in the caption.

In the audit this cut is drawn from, 20 real buyer questions were run identically across engines. The same brand came back as the top recommendation on several of them, and did not appear at all on another.

## Notes worth reading before posting

- **Cut 5 and cut 9 both link to the Chicago city page.** That page carries a
  page level superlative claim that does not hold up, flagged earlier in this
  campaign after it reached two rendered cuts. Nothing in these captions quotes
  it. The prompt, the ranks, the collection date and the invented name finding
  are separate and are traced to the page's own tables. If the page copy gets
  corrected, these captions are unaffected.
- **No caption names a company or a person that turned up inside a result set.**
  Cut 5 says "the same company" and cut 9 says "a firm name", deliberately.
  Naming the engine being measured is fine and is done throughout.
- **Cut 4 carries no engine count.** The audit it draws on names a lineup that is
  not the current one, so the caption says "several" and "another", matching the
  video.
- **No caption carries a price**, since all nine are top of funnel.
- **"Link in bio"** is the closing line in every caption because Instagram
  captions do not carry live links. The first comment block holds the real URL.
  If you would rather not use a first comment, the bio link still works and the
  caption reads the same.
