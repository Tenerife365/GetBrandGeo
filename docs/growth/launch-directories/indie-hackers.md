## Full description

```
BrandGEO runs a brand's real buyer questions against up to seven AI engines
(ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews,
Grok) and reports whether the brand is mentioned, how it ranks against named
competitors, and what sentiment the answer carries.

I built it because the tools doing this well are priced for enterprise
marketing teams. BrandGEO has a free tier to check your own visibility today,
and Radar, a EUR 29/month entry tier (EUR 39 after launch), for a smaller
business or solo operator who wants to monitor this ongoing rather than
check once.

Built and run solo from the Canary Islands, Spain.
```
