## Detailed Description (already delivered and pasted, no character cap on
this field per the live form: reproduced here for the record)

```
What BrandGEO does

BrandGEO monitors how your brand shows up when people ask AI assistants for
recommendations. It runs your brand's real buyer questions (the kind
customers actually type, like "best CRM for small teams" or "best moving
company in Austin") against up to seven AI engines: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude,
Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews, and Grok.

For each question, on each engine, BrandGEO records whether your brand is
mentioned, where it ranks against competitors, what sentiment the answer
carries, and which competitor names show up when yours doesn't.

Key features

- Multi-engine tracking: up to seven AI engines checked on the same buyer
  prompts, so you see where you're covered and where you're invisible.
- Competitor visibility: every run also captures which named competitors the
  engines recommend instead of you.
- Brand sentiment: positive, neutral, or negative tone tracked per engine and
  per prompt, with the response snippet that produced it.
- Recommendations: specific, prompt-level suggestions for closing the gaps
  the monitoring finds.
- Self-serve and managed tiers: run it yourself starting from a free plan, or
  have it run for you on the higher tiers.

What sets it apart

Most tools built for this category are priced and designed for enterprise
marketing teams with dedicated budget. BrandGEO's entry tiers (a free plan,
and Radar at EUR 29 a month at launch, EUR 39 after) are built for a smaller
business or solo operator who wants to know what AI is telling their
customers about them, without needing a growth team to justify the spend.
```
