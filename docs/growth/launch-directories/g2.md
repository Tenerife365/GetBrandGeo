## Reviews: real users only, this is a hard rule, not a preference

G2's own "Review Generation" feature (inviting real, existing customers by
email to leave a review after they've used the product) is legitimate and
standard practice on G2, it is not the same thing as a solicited fake. The
rule this session was given is specific: **never buy, fabricate, or
incentivize a review that isn't from a real user who actually used BrandGEO.**
In practice that means:

- Do not request reviews until there are real, paying (or at minimum real,
  active free-tier) customers who have used the product.
- When inviting, use G2's own review-invite flow addressed to a real client
  by name, never a generic blast to people who haven't used it.
- Never offer payment, discount, or any incentive tied to a review being
  positive. A neutral thank-you gift offered equally regardless of the
  review's content is the only defensible variant, and even that should wait
  until there's a real base of users to invite.

This step stays open until there are real customers to invite: it is not
part of this week's static-listing push.
