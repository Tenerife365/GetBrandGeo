## Repost commentary

The instinct almost everyone has when they first try this is to ask AI about their own brand by name.

I understand the instinct. It feels like the natural first move, and it almost always produces a flattering answer, because nearly anything with a website clears the bar of "has this model heard of you." I have watched people run the test this way, feel reassured by a warm answer, and stop right there.

The version that actually tells you something never mentions your name at all. It is the question a buyer types before they know you exist. That version is less comfortable to run, because sometimes you are not in the list, and there is no polite LLM answer to soften that.

One more thing worth admitting. Doing this properly, in two languages, across three engines, on the same day, by hand, takes real effort. Ten minutes is an honest number for one category. Doing it for the dozen categories that actually matter to a real business, then doing it again next month to see whether anything moved, stops being a ten minute job. That gap between the quick version and the version that is actually useful is most of why we ended up building something around it, though that is not really what this post is about.
