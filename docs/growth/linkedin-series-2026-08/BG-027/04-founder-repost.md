## Repost commentary

This study found a flaw in our own product, so I want to be the one to say it.

Until recently BrandGEO reported one AI visibility score per brand. One number, one list of engines, one competitor set. That design quietly assumes the answer an engine gives is a property of your brand.

We tested that assumption across four European cities, asking each commercial question twice, once in the local language and once in English, on the same day, of the same engines.

Of 486 companies the engines named, 399 appeared in one language and never in the other.

In thirteen of forty-six comparisons, the two answers had no company in common at all. Not a lower ranking. A different set of competitors entirely.

Which means the number we were reporting was the answer to a question we had not asked properly: in which language?

I would rather publish that than quietly patch it. If you sell outside English and you are measuring your AI visibility in English, you are reading a report about a market you may not sell into.
