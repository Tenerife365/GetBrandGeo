## Post

Ask whatever tool measures your AI visibility one question: when they say they monitor Google, which Google.

Google AI Mode is a tab a searcher switches to on purpose. Google AI Overviews is the summary block shown by default at the top of an ordinary results page, reaching a far larger share of searches. They are two different products and they disagree often enough that reporting one as "Google" hides the surface most people actually see.

We wrote up why we measure both separately, why we retired Meta AI in July for answering from training data only, why DeepSeek stays out for the same reason, and the four questions worth asking any vendor who advertises an engine count without saying what was actually switched on when they queried it.
