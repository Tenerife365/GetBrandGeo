Four fixes that remove a blocker outright, from the checklist behind our multilingual AI visibility study.

One. Your second language pages have to be real, fetchable URLs. Not a JavaScript switcher rewriting the same page.

Two. Reciprocal hreflang tags, each language pointing at the others and at itself.

Three. Structured data in the language of the page it sits on. We found three of our own FAQPage blocks silently invalid for weeks over one missing brace.

Four. One spelling of your own name, everywhere. Our study returned "Only You Boutique Hotel" and "Only YOU Boutique Hotel Madrid" as two separate strings from the same run.

None of these require a rebuild. All four are things whose absence stops an engine from having the information at all, not things that merely make it prefer someone else.
