One category in our four city bilingual study barely split by language at all.

Rome real estate agencies, through Perplexity: 9 of 10 names matched across Italian and English. Every other category we measured split hard, most of them losing 80% or more of their names to the other language.

What was different: those firms were documented consistently in both languages, by sources other than themselves, under a name that never changed.

That turns into a checklist. If you only do three things: make sure your second language pages are real, fetchable URLs, put your structured data in the language of the page it sits on, and use one spelling of your own name everywhere. None of the three require a rebuild.
