The question was "Top-rated property management companies in Chicago". ChatGPT first, Claude first, Perplexity second, Gemini fourth, and Google AI Mode surfaced the company by name without giving it a position at all.

The Gemini row is the useful one. A rank tracker hands you a single number to defend. This run handed back five separate answers, and being present in them is a different question from being placed in them.
