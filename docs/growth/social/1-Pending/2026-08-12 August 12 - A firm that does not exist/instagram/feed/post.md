Three mistakes that will make you draw the wrong conclusion from this test, before you even run it.

One. Reading a single run as a measurement. These systems are not deterministic. The same question, asked twice, can come back different.

Two. Counting a blank or errored answer as a zero. In our own four city study, ChatGPT errored on all 32 prompts on the collection day. We excluded it entirely rather than record it as a dramatic result, because a missing measurement is not the same thing as an absence.

Three. Assuming your customers' local language is automatically the one that matters. Check who actually buys from you, not who you assume does.

The test itself takes about ten minutes. Getting a real answer out of it depends on avoiding these three.
