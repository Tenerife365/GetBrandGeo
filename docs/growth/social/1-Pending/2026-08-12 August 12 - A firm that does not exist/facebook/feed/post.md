You can test whether AI recommends your brand in your customers' own language in about ten minutes. No tool, no budget.

Write the question a real buyer types, not your brand name. Translate it as literally as the two languages allow. Ask both versions, the same day, on at least three engines.

Three matters more than it sounds like it should. In our own study across four European cities, one engine alone produced five comparisons where the two language answers shared zero companies in common. A single engine can make the gap look far bigger, or far smaller, than it really is.

Write down both lists of companies named, not just your impression of them. Then check whether you are on both, or only one.
