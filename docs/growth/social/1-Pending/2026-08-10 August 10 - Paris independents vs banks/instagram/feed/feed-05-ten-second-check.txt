The check from this week's Reel, as a card you can screenshot.

One. Open ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Perplexity.

Two. Type what a buyer would ask. Not your name. Something like best what you sell in your city.

Three. Read the answer back and count the names sitting above yours.

That is the whole check. One engine, one day, no account, no cost. It tells you something real, and it also tells you very little on its own, which is the part most people stop before reaching.

A single answer is a snapshot. Ask the same question on a second engine and see whether they agree before you draw a conclusion from either one.
