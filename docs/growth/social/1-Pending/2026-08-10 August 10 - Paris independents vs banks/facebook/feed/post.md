We put one question to AI twice in Madrid, once in Spanish and once in English.

Hotels near Madrid-Barajas, one collection day, 10 July 2026. One engine gave a full, real answer in English and nothing usable in Spanish. Another engine did the exact reverse on the identical prompt: a real answer in Spanish, nothing in English.

There is no engine count on that finding, deliberately. A further engine failed to collect on every Madrid prompt in that run, and the page reports it as a data gap rather than folding it into a result.

If you sell in more than one language, a clean result in one of them is weak evidence about the other.
