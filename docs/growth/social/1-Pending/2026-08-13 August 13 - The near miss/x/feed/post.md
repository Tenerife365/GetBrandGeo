Part 1 of 4:

Madrid was the one city in our four city bilingual study where the language barely mattered. One category explains almost all of it.

Part 2 of 4:

Hotels near Madrid-Barajas airport. Crowne Plaza, Hilton, Barajas Plaza, Clement Barajas, Maydrit, Ibis and Meliá were named in both Spanish and English.

Part 3 of 4:

The reason is not linguistic. The category is dominated by international chains whose names are identical in every language and are described the same way by the same sources everywhere. There is nothing for the language to change.

Part 4 of 4:

Chain-dominated categories converge across languages. Independent-dominated categories diverge. The independent business is the one most likely to have this gap, and the least likely to be checking for it. https://getbrandgeo.com/bg-027.html?utm_source=x&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=pulse
