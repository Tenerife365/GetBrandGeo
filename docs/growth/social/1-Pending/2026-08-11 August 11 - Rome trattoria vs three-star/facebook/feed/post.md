We asked AI for a restaurant for a business dinner in Rome, once in Italian and once in English.

The Italian answer: Trapizzino, Supplizio, Osteria Fratelli Mori, Glass Hostaria, Alla Bottega. Local spots, some of them casual.

The English answer: La Pergola, Salumeria Roscioli, Pierluigi, Dal Bolognese, Enoteca la Torre. La Pergola alone carries three Michelin stars.

Same city, same question, same day. Two different restaurants entirely, because the language you type in already picked the shortlist for you.

Across the whole Rome data set, 133 companies were named and 81.2% of them showed up in only one of the two languages.

If you run a business that lives or dies on which of those two lists it appears in, this is worth checking for yourself.
