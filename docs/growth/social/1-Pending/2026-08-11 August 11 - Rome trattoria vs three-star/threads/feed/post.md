Following up on the Rome post from earlier today: the trattoria and three star split is not random, and it is not really about restaurants.

Across our four city study, the categories that converge across languages are the ones dominated by international chains. Madrid airport hotels named the same names in Spanish and English because Crowne Plaza, Hilton and Meliá read identically in every language and are described the same way by the same sources everywhere.

Categories built from independents split hard, because there is nothing forcing the two languages to reach for the same source.

So the useful question is not "does my business appear in AI answers." It is "is my category the kind that converges or the kind that diverges," and if it is the second kind, whether you have checked both languages at all.
