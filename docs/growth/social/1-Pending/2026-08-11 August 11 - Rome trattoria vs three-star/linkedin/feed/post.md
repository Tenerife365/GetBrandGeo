Ask an AI for a restaurant for a business dinner in Rome, in Italian and then in English. Same day, same question, translated.

Italian: Trapizzino, Supplizio, Osteria Fratelli Mori, Glass Hostaria, Alla Bottega.

English: La Pergola, Salumeria Roscioli, Pierluigi, Dal Bolognese, Enoteca la Torre. La Pergola alone holds three Michelin stars.

Rome, across our whole four-category study: 133 companies named, 81.2% of them in only one of the two languages.

This is not really about restaurants. It is a pattern that held across all four cities we measured. Categories built from international chains converge across languages, because the same names read the same way everywhere. Categories built from independents diverge, because nothing forces the two languages toward the same source.

If your category is the second kind, the local-language answer and the English answer are effectively two separate marketplaces, and being visible in one tells you nothing about the other.

Full study and the city-by-city numbers in the first comment.
