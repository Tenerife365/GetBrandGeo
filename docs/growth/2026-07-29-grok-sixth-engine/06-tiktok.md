# 06. TikTok (TOFU)

**Driver:** Loss aversion. **38 seconds.**
Native tone. This is not the Reel with a different logo. TikTok gets a person
talking, direct to camera, with the graphics as support rather than the subject.

| TIME | ON SCREEN | SPOKEN |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0-1.5s | Person mid-sentence, already talking, no intro. Text slams on: **"you're paying for engines that don't work"** | "You are probably paying for AI engines that never actually looked at the internet." |
| 1.5-5s | Person continues. Small dashboard mock floats bottom-right showing a logo grid, seven logos, three of them dimming out one by one. | "Every tool in this space sells you a number. Five engines, seven, twelve." |
| 5-11s | Cut to screen recording. A prompt is typed: "best commercial caterers in Chicago". Two answer panels populate side by side. | "But some of those engines are not searching anything. They are answering from memory, from whenever the model finished training." |
| 11-17s | Left panel highlighted green, labelled SEARCHED. Right panel highlighted amber, labelled MEMORY. Two of the ranked names differ. Circle drawn around the difference. | "Which means a business that opened this year does not exist to it, and one that closed two years ago is still sitting in the top three." |
| 17-23s | Back to person. Text: **"and it looks exactly the same"** | "And here is the part that gets people. The output looks identical. Same layout, same confidence, same clean percentage on your dashboard." |
| 23-30s | Screen recording of a pricing page. An engine logo is deleted from a row. Counter ticks from 6 down to 5. | "We build one of these tools. Two weeks ago we deleted an engine off our own pricing page because it had no web search. Our own number went down." |
| 30-35s | Seven engine marks resolve in a row, each with a check. Two highlighted violet, labelled "reads X too" and "the block above the links". | "We are at seven now. Two went in today. One reads X on top of the web, one reads the AI summary Google puts above the links by default. All seven actually search." |
| 35-38s | Person, direct to camera. Text: **"ask them which ones searched"** | "So ask whoever sells you this. Which of your engines actually searched? Watch how long that takes to answer." |

## Notes

- **Do not reuse the Reel's voiceover.** TikTok punishes content that reads as
  cross-posted, and the Reel is narrated over graphics while this is a person
  talking. Different asset, same argument.
- Cold open, mid-sentence, no logo bumper. The first frame decides this.
- The screen recording at 5 to 17 seconds is the proof beat and it needs to be a
  real capture, not a mock. If a real side-by-side is not available before the
  send gate clears, hold this asset specifically rather than faking the panels.
- Burned-in captions throughout. Do not rely on TikTok auto-captions for the
  SEARCHED and MEMORY labels; those two words carry the whole piece.
- **Safe area.** Keep every burned-in element inside the usable band from
  `docs/growth/channel-specs-2026-07-29.md`. The 11 to 17 second beat is the proof
  shot and its SEARCHED and MEMORY labels are the two words that cannot be
  obscured. Place them in the upper half of the frame, not near the bottom where
  TikTok's own UI stacks.
- **Posting is blocked, and it is not a code fix.** Programmatic posting requires
  the app to pass TikTok's audit. Until it does, everything posted by an unaudited
  client is restricted to private viewing. That is a review process with lead
  time. **Plan on posting this by hand.**
- Caption under the post, 2 hashtags maximum, TikTok is not a hashtag channel:
  > we deleted an engine off our own pricing page. here is why.
  > #ai #seo
- No link in caption. Link in bio.
- **Hold until the send gate clears.**
