# 04. Short-form social

Three assets. Different drivers on purpose, so they do not read as one post
pasted three times.

---

## 4a. X, standalone quote post (TOFU)

**Driver:** Status threat. **238 characters.**
Paired visual: **V1**.

> Everyone in AI visibility competes on engine count.
>
> We removed one from our own pricing page on 16 July because it answered from
> training data with no web search.
>
> Seven engines now. All seven actually retrieve.
>
> Ask your vendor which of theirs do.

**Posting note:** standalone, not attached to the thread. Runs two days after the
thread so it does not cannibalise it. No link.

---

## 4b. Threads (TOFU)

**Driver:** Curiosity gap. **138 words.**
Paired visual: **V1**.

> There is a question you can ask any AI visibility tool that most of them cannot
> answer cleanly.
>
> Not "how many engines do you monitor." That number is easy to raise and tells
> you almost nothing.
>
> Ask: when you queried those engines for my report, was web search switched on?
>
> A model with no retrieval answers from training data. You get a clean list, a
> tidy percentage, and a picture of what was true before its cutoff. Anything that
> launched this year is invisible to it. The output looks identical to an engine
> that actually went and looked, which is the part that gets people.
>
> We cut an engine from our own product over this two weeks ago. Engine count went
> down. It was still the right call.
>
> What would your tool say?

**Posting note:** the closing question is doing real work on Threads, where reply
volume drives distribution harder than on X. Reply to every reply for the first
two hours.

---

## 4c. Facebook (MOFU)

**Driver:** Loss aversion. **112 words.**
Paired visual: **V2**, with link preview to the blog post.

> If you have ever run a brand visibility report and thought the results looked
> slightly out of date, there is a decent chance they were.
>
> Some AI engines answer from training data rather than searching the web. The
> report looks the same either way: same format, same confidence, same percentage
> at the top. The difference is that one of them is describing today and the other
> is describing whenever the model finished training.
>
> We wrote up why we removed an engine from our own product over this, why we
> turned down two more, and the four questions worth asking whoever sells you AI
> visibility.

**Link:** blog post URL, set at publish.

---

## Notes

- Never run 4a and 4b within the same 24 hours. Threads and X share an audience
  at the top of this category and the near-duplicate reads badly.
- 4c is the only one of the three carrying a link, because Facebook is the only
  one of the three where an outbound link does not materially suppress reach.
- **Hold all three until the send gate clears.**
