# BrandGEO partner program: affiliate guide

Short version for partners. The public page, the terms and the FAQ are at
`https://getbrandgeo.com/affiliates.html` and
`https://getbrandgeo.com/affiliate-terms.html`.

## Joining

Either you receive an invitation email from us, or you apply on the public
page (name, email, company, website or social profile, country, how you plan
to promote, preferred payout method, and the terms and privacy boxes). We
review applications by hand and reply by email. Approval is per program: you
can be in several programs, each with its own link and its own rules.

The invitation link is valid for seven days and can be used once. It shows
the programs you were invited to, their commission rules and the terms; you
accept, choose how you want to be paid (Wise, Revolut, PayPal, bank transfer
or other), and you are signed in. We only keep the identifiers a transfer
needs (account holder, email, IBAN, tag). Never a password, never a card.

## Signing in

`https://app.getbrandgeo.com/affiliate/login`, your email, a magic link. No
password to remember. The link signs you into your dashboard at
`https://app.getbrandgeo.com/affiliate`.

## Your dashboard

- **Programs.** For each program you are in: your referral link, your code,
  any coupon codes, the commission rules, the payout terms, and the
  promotional resources we provide. Copy the link with one click.
- **Stats.** Clicks, leads, sales, and the commission totals per currency by
  status.
- **Conversions.** Every lead and sale attributed to you: date, type, a
  masked customer reference (never the customer's email or order details),
  amount and status.
- **Commissions.** Each with its status:
  `pending` (inside the approval delay, usually 30 days, so a refund can
  still cancel it), `approved` (owed), `payable` (in a payout being
  prepared), `paid` (with the date), `reversed` (the sale was refunded) or
  `rejected` (with the reason).
- **Payouts.** Each payout with its date, total, method and the transfer
  reference. You can attach your invoice to a payout.
- **Settings.** Your profile and payout details.

## How tracking works

Your link is `https://app.getbrandgeo.com/r/<program>/<YOURCODE>`. It sends
the visitor to the program's site with your code attached. The site keeps it
for the attribution window (30 days for BrandGEO) and it is added to the
signup and the checkout. If the visitor refuses analytics cookies, the
referral is kept for that browser session only.

Attribution rules, in plain words: the last affiliate link a customer
clicked wins (some programs use the first); a coupon code typed at checkout
beats a link; once a customer has bought, they stay yours for the recurring
period; a customer who is you, your company or your own domain is flagged
and reviewed.

Add your own UTM parameters to the link if you want to see which channel
converts; we keep them.

## How you get paid

Commissions are approved after the program's approval delay, provided the
sale was not refunded. Once your approved balance in a currency reaches the
program minimum, we create a payout, transfer it through the method you chose,
and mark it paid with the reference. You get an email at each step: new
conversion, commission approved, payout completed.

Amounts are in the program's currency, exact to the cent. We do not convert
currencies. Taxes on what you receive are your responsibility; attach an
invoice to the payout if you need one on record.

## Rules

Do not bid on the BrandGEO brand in paid search, do not send unsolicited
email, do not refer yourself or your own company, do not promise features or
prices that are not on the site. The full terms are on the terms page and
are versioned; if they change, you will be asked to accept the new version.
