## Before this is sent

Run every line. A no on any of them means it does not go.

- [ ] Every number is this client's own measured result. Nothing illustrative,
      nothing borrowed from another account.
- [ ] The low confidence flag on the run was false.
- [ ] The date and the engine list are at the top.
- [ ] Every competitor named is quoted from the report, not remembered.
- [ ] "Could not be reached" and "did not name you" are still separate.
- [ ] The limits section is filled in and specific.
- [ ] No em dashes, no en dashes.
- [ ] None of the banned words. See the house style in the pack README.
- [ ] It fits on one page.
- [ ] The person who owns this relationship has read it and said yes.

If the client says a result is wrong: agree, then check. "You may be right,
engines move, here is the exact question we asked and the date." Never defend
the number. Re-run it and send what comes back.
