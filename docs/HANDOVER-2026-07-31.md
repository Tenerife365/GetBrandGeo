## 6. Traps this session paid for. Do not re-learn these.

1. **A check that reads the working tree can pass while production is broken.**
   The logo fix existed on disk, uncommitted, for an unknown period. The check
   was green the whole time. Always confirm a fix is in `HEAD`.
2. **Enforcement lives in Postgres as often as in JavaScript.** I told Constantin
   `PLAN_PROMPTS` was enforced nowhere, having grepped only the TypeScript.
   `trg_enforce_prompt_cap` has been enforcing it in the database all along.
   **Grep the database too.**
3. **Source-regex test assertions are defeated by commenting a line out.** Two
   rounds of mutation testing used deletions only and both missed it. The worst
   case left `expire-plan-grants` reverting every client with a past grant date,
   with the harness fully green.
4. **The same fix in one of two places is the commonest bug here.** The AI
   Overviews exclusion existed in one page, so two screens reported different
   numbers for the same client at the same instant.
5. **A vendor's own assistant will confidently describe UI that does not exist.**
   Verify against an endpoint, not against what a product says about itself.
6. **BrandGEO and TalentWeLove crossed over three times in one day** — pasted
   requirements, the Stripe account, and a Netlify build log. Candidates, ATS,
   recruiting or Next.js means you are in the wrong repo.
