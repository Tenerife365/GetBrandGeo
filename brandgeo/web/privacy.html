1. Who We Are

BrandGEO ("we", "us", "our") is an AI Visibility Intelligence service operating under the domain getbrandgeo.com, based in Santa Cruz de Tenerife, Canary Islands, Spain (European Union).

2. What Data We Collect

From the public website, only what you voluntarily submit through our contact or audit request form:

  • Full name
  • Business email address
  • Company or brand name
  • Industry
  • Optional message or notes

From the dashboard, if you hold an account, we additionally process:

  • Account details: your email address, your role, and the client account you belong to
  • Brand configuration: brand name, aliases, website and named competitors
  • The monitoring prompts you create, and the responses AI engines return for them
  • Support tickets and any messages you send us through the dashboard
  • Billing status. Payment is handled by Stripe and we never see or store full card numbers.

We do not collect special category data under Article 9 GDPR, and our services are not directed at children.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to:

  • Respond to your audit request or enquiry
  • Deliver the free AI visibility audit you requested
  • Follow up regarding BrandGEO services if you expressed interest

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process your data on the basis of legitimate interest (responding to a service enquiry you initiated) and, where applicable, consent (when you opt in to further communication).

5. Data Retention

We apply the following retention periods:

  • Contact enquiries: retained for 24 months from the date of enquiry, or until you request deletion, whichever comes first.
  • AI visibility results (ai_results): retained for 24 months from the date of collection, then automatically and permanently deleted. This allows clients to analyse 2 years of brand visibility trends while ensuring data does not accumulate indefinitely.
  • Client account data: retained for the duration of the active subscription plus 90 days after termination, to allow for data export. Deleted permanently thereafter upon written request.
  • User accounts: deleted upon written request to gdpr@getbrandgeo.com.

Retention is enforced automatically. You may request early deletion of your data at any time.

7. Cookies & Analytics

Full detail, including a cookie-by-cookie schedule and retention periods, is in our Cookie Policy. In summary:

Google Analytics is loaded only if you consent. When you first visit getbrandgeo.com you are asked whether to allow it. Until you accept, no request is made to Google and no analytics cookies are set. If you accept, Google Analytics sets the _ga and _ga_<id> cookies. If you decline, they are never set and the Google script is never loaded at all. Accepting and declining are offered with equal prominence, nothing is pre-selected, and continuing to browse is not treated as consent. You can change your answer at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer of any page, and we ask again at least every 12 months.

Plausible Analytics runs for every visitor and requires no consent. It sets no cookies, stores no identifier on your device and collects no personal data, processing only aggregate traffic figures such as page views, referrer, country and device type. Because it neither stores nor accesses information on your device, the consent requirement in Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive does not apply. See plausible.io/data-policy.

We use no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Google advertising signals are held at "denied" at all times, including for visitors who accept analytics, so no advertising profile is built and no audience pixel fires.

The dashboard (app.getbrandgeo.com) uses only strictly necessary authentication storage and your own interface preferences, such as theme and language. It carries no analytics and no advertising technology of any kind.

8. Your Rights Under GDPR

As a data subject in the EU, you have the right to:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification: request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure: request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restriction: request that we limit how we process your data
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interest

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at gdpr@getbrandgeo.com. We respond within one month, extendable by two further months for complex requests, and we will tell you if an extension applies. Exercising your rights is free of charge.
