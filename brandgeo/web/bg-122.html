What Search Console Shows You About AI Search Today

Search Console's generative AI report counts impressions in AI Overviews and AI Mode, and nothing else. The UK competition regulator has told Google to give publishers clicks and click-through rate for its AI features by 3 December. How to read the report now, and what to look for after the deadline.

If you have opened Search Console in the past few months, you may have seen a separate report for generative AI. It tells you how often links to your site appeared in Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode. It does not tell you how often anyone clicked them.

That may change soon, at least in the UK. The Competition and Markets Authority has told Google to give publishers impressions, clicks and click-through rate for its search AI features, and it set 3 December 2026 as the date. Here is what the report shows today, how to read it, and what to check once the deadline passes.

Sources, and what they cannot tell you

We read Google's announcement of the report (3 June 2026), the Search Console help page for it, the CMA's summary of the publisher conduct requirement, and Search Engine Journal's report of 2 November 2026, all on 6 November 2026. Nobody outside Google knows yet what the new metrics will look like, where they will appear, or whether they will reach accounts outside the UK.

What the report shows today

Google announced the generative AI performance report on 3 June 2026, first for a test group of sites. By its own note, it reached all websites worldwide on 31 August 2026.

QuestionWhat Google's pages say
Which featuresAI Overviews and AI Mode in Search; generative AI features in Discover have their own report
Which metricImpressions: how often links to your site were shown in a generative AI feature
Broken down byPages, countries, devices (Search only) and dates

Left outSearch Labs experiments

Google also says the same data is still included in the overall Performance report. So an AI impression is not new traffic on top of your normal numbers. It is part of them, shown again in its own view.

How to read it without fooling yourself

Start with pages, not totals. The page breakdown tells you which of your URLs Google shows inside AI answers. Check whether they are the pages you would want a buyer to land on. A page you would never send a buyer to, such as an old blog post or an outdated price list, showing up there is worth noticing.

Compare with the same pages' clicks. Open the normal Performance report for the same pages and period. If a page has many AI impressions and the same clicks as before, the report alone cannot tell you whether the AI answer replaced the click or simply added a mention.

Mind the small print. The help page lists limits worth knowing before you put a number in a client report: the newest days are preliminary and shown as a dotted line; chart and table totals can differ; values shown as "~" or "-" download as zeros in an export; tables stop at 1,000 rows; and the report may not appear at all for a site with too few AI impressions or one that has excluded itself from these features.

What the UK regulator asked for

The CMA's publisher conduct requirement says Google must give publishers transparency metrics for search AI features, including impressions, clicks and click-through rate, aggregated across features such as AI Overviews and AI Mode, along with referral data so publishers can judge the traffic those features send. The deadline is 3 December 2026. Google then has three more months to build a page-level control, and the CMA says it expects changes to start sooner.

According to Search Engine Journal, Google's first compliance report covers 3 June to 3 December and is due on 3 January 2027. Asked about adding AI click data to Search Console, Google's search team said it had "nothing to announce yet".

What to look for after 3 December

CheckWhy it matters
Does a clicks column appear in the generative AI report?It is the number the requirement names and the one missing today
Is it visible on a site outside the UK?The requirement is British; a worldwide rollout is Google's choice
Is AI Mode reported apart from AI Overviews?The requirement allows figures aggregated across features
Does the page-level control appear by early March 2027?It is the second step of the same requirement

Write down your current AI impression numbers for your main pages before the deadline. If clicks arrive, you will want a before and after, measured the same way.

What neither version will tell you

Even with clicks, the report measures links. It will not show what the AI answer said about you, which competitors it named next to you, or whether it got your hours, fees or address right. For a local business those are often the parts that decide the call. Our own studies of 591 AI answers to local buyer questions found engines quoting star ratings, years in business and fees for the firms they named, and in some cases naming no firm at all. None of that shows up as an impression.

What to do with this

This week: open the generative AI report, note the pages that collect AI impressions and their numbers, and keep a copy. After 3 December: check whether clicks and click-through rate appear, and whether they appear for your country.

Alongside it: ask the engines the questions your buyers ask and read the answers. Search Console tells you that you were shown. Only the answer tells you how you were described.
