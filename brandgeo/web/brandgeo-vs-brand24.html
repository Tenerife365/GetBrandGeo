BrandGEO vs Brand24

Brand24 tracks what people publish about your brand: social posts, news, blogs, podcasts and reviews. BrandGEO tracks what AI engines answer when a buyer asks them for a recommendation. Brand24 also sells AI visibility, through its sister product Chatbeat, so this page compares all three.

Brand24 and Chatbeat details read from their own websites on 6 November 2026. Their prices are in US dollars, BrandGEO's in euros, as each company lists them.

Choose BrandGEO if…

You need to know whether AI engines recommend you

Your question is “when a buyer asks ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI for options in my category, are we on the list, and who is named instead?” You want to start from €0, and keep the option of having the work done for you.

Choose Brand24 if…

You need to hear what people say about you online

Your question is “who is talking about us, where, and in what tone?” You want mentions across social media, news, blogs, podcasts and reviews, with updates from every 12 hours up to real time.

Why this page exists: when we asked seven AI engines “Best tools for tracking brand reputation online” on 6 November 2026, at least five of them named Brand24. Buyers who search for an AI visibility tool often meet it there, so it is worth being clear about what each product actually measures.

● The honest version

Where Brand24 and Chatbeat win

  • Far wider listening. Brand24 covers social media, news, blogs, podcasts and reviews in 108 languages, which BrandGEO does not try to do.
  • Faster updates. Brand24 refreshes hourly to real time on most plans, and Chatbeat lists a daily refresh. BrandGEO refreshes weekly.
  • More prompts at the entry price. Chatbeat Essential gives 30 prompts for $99; BrandGEO Essentials gives 18 for €99.
  • Citation analysis and DeepSeek. Chatbeat shows which sources AI answers cite, and lists DeepSeek among its engines.

Where BrandGEO wins

  • A lower start. A Free plan at €0 and Radar at €29 a month, against a $99 Chatbeat entry plan and $199 for Brand24.
  • Google's AI answers by name. Google AI Mode from Growth and Google AI Overviews from Growth PRO, alongside ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and Grok.
  • Someone to do the work. Managed runs the research, audit and reporting for you; neither Brand24 nor Chatbeat lists a done-for-you plan.
  • From finding to fixing. Each gap becomes a ranked fix, and Growth plans add a site audit with fix drafts.
  • Published method. BrandGEO's research is public, with two records on Zenodo carrying permanent DOIs.
● Common questions

Is Brand24 an AI visibility tool?

Brand24 describes itself as an AI social listening tool: it tracks mentions of a brand across social media, news, blogs, videos, forums, podcasts and reviews. Its AI visibility product is Chatbeat, which Brand24 calls its sister product and sells on its own or as a bundle with Brand24.

Which is cheaper, BrandGEO or Brand24?

For AI answer monitoring, BrandGEO starts lower: a Free plan at 0 euros and Radar at 29 euros a month (launch price). Chatbeat, Brand24's AI visibility product, lists its Essential plan at $99 a month for 30 prompts. Brand24's social listening plans start at $199 a month billed yearly, or $249 billed monthly. At around the same entry price, Chatbeat gives more prompts (30 for $99) than BrandGEO Essentials (18 for 99 euros).

Which AI engines does each one track?

BrandGEO tracks up to seven: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Grok, Google AI Mode and Google AI Overviews, depending on the plan. Chatbeat's AI visibility page names ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, DeepSeek and Grok. Brand24's social listening plans monitor online sources such as social media and news, not AI engine answers.

Should I use Brand24 and BrandGEO together?

They answer different questions, so some teams use both. Brand24 shows what people publish about you. BrandGEO shows whether AI engines name you when a buyer asks for a recommendation, and which competitors they name instead. If you only need one, pick the question that costs you more customers today.
