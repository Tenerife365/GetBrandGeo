AI Visibility in Tampa: A Team Breaks the Pattern Again

We ran 6 real Tampa categories (personal injury law, real estate, property management, immigration law, insurance/financial services law, and hotels/hospitality) through the same AI Visibility pipeline BrandGEO runs for paying clients, across five AI engines. Tampa is genuinely different in shape from most cities researched: no category reached 4 of 5 engines. Its strongest results all sit at exactly 3 of 5.

The standout finding: the Kendall Bonner Team became only the second real estate team in this entire research program to reach real cross-engine consensus, after Atlanta's Justin Landis Group, breaking a pattern that holds almost everywhere else.

Only the second real estate team to break the pattern

The Kendall Bonner Team was ChatGPT's prose #1 pick, named by Google AI Mode among its top options, and Perplexity's #1 structured pick. Every other city researched except Atlanta showed individual real estate agents and teams as heavily or completely fragmented. Tampa is the second exception.

An unusually precise two-engine match

For "best insurance and financial services law firms in Tampa," ChatGPT and Gemini didn't just agree on the same firms, they agreed on the exact same three names in the exact same order: Dutton Law Group, P.A. at #1, Mills Law Group at #2, and Mubarak, Sherif & Oladipo, PLLC at #3. Claude, Google AI Mode, and Perplexity all diverged completely, so this only counts as 2/5 by engine agreement, but the identical three-firm sequence between two independent engines is a notably precise match.

A data-quality note, not a finding

All 5 engines returned usable data on every prompt this run, no collection errors. As in Miami, 4 of 5 engines answered the "hotels and hospitality management companies" prompt almost entirely with hotel properties rather than actual management firms; kept as reported since the named businesses are real. This run used Google AI Mode in place of the now-retired Meta AI engine used in earlier city pages.

What this means if your business is in Tampa

  1. 1

    No category here is locked down

    Unlike cities where one or two brands sweep a category outright, Tampa's strongest results all sit at 3/5. Every category tested still has room for a new name to enter the consensus set.

  2. 2

    If you're an independent real estate agent, you're now up against a team with real traction

    The Kendall Bonner Team broke a pattern that holds in almost every other city. An independent agent in Tampa faces tougher competition for AI visibility than in most markets tested.

  3. 3

    Personal injury law and immigration law are the easiest doors in

    Neither category has a name crossing 2/5, and immigration law shows zero overlap at all. That's the best-documented opportunity in Tampa for a specific firm to become the AI-recommended name.

  4. 4

    Re-run this regularly, not once

    These answers reflect what the engines say today, this week. Engine behavior and extraction quality both shift between runs. A one-time check tells you less than an ongoing one.

● Common Questions

Which brand had the strongest AI consensus in Tampa?

No category reached 4 of 5 engines. The strongest results all sit at exactly 3 of 5: the Kendall Bonner Team for real estate, Hoffman Realty and WrightDavis Property Management for property management, and three hotel names for hospitality.

Do real estate agents usually reach cross-engine AI consensus?

No, almost never. Tampa's Kendall Bonner Team reaching 3 of 5 engines puts it in a small group. As of 2026-07-30 the only other clean team-level convergences in this program are Atlanta's Justin Landis Group at 4 of 5, and Charlotte's Stone Realty Group and Andy Bovender Team, which both reached 3 of 5 in the same run Tampa was collected in. Everywhere else the category was heavily or completely fragmented.

Did any AI engines produce an unusually precise match in Tampa?

Yes. For insurance and financial services law, ChatGPT and Gemini both named the exact same three firms in the exact same order, Dutton Law Group, Mills Law Group, and Mubarak, Sherif & Oladipo, an unusually precise two-engine agreement even though the other three engines diverged completely.
