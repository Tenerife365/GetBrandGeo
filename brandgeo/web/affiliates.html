The details

Commission, payouts, eligibility

How commission is calculated

On the program's rules, on our servers, from the amount the customer actually paid. Refunds and cancellations reverse the commission. A pending commission becomes approved after the program's approval period, so a chargeback cannot be paid out twice.

When you get paid

  • Approved commissions are grouped into a payout once you pass the program minimum.
  • Payouts go out monthly by Wise, Revolut, PayPal or bank transfer, in the program currency.
  • You see every click, lead, sale, commission and payout in your affiliate dashboard.

Who can join

  • Anyone who can send us relevant traffic: agencies, consultants, newsletter writers, YouTubers, community owners, SaaS partners.
  • No referring yourself, your own company, or people you bill directly for BrandGEO.
  • No paid brand-term bidding, coupon scraping sites, spam, or misleading claims.

FAQ

Questions partners ask

How is a sale attributed to me?

By the last referral link the customer clicked within the program's attribution window, or by your coupon code at checkout, whichever applies. A coupon code always wins over a link. If the customer arrives without cookies, the code still works, and we can also attribute by hand when a customer tells us who sent them.

Do I earn on renewals?

Where the program says so. BrandGEO pays 10% of each renewal for 12 months from the first payment. Programs with a fixed amount per sale or per lead pay once.

What happens on a refund?

The commission is reversed. If it was already paid out, it is flagged and deducted from your next payout. We never claw back money by other means.

Can I refer my own company?

No. Self-referrals and referrals of companies you invoice directly for BrandGEO services are flagged automatically and not paid.

Do you pay in my currency?

Payouts are made in the program's currency (EUR for BrandGEO). Your bank or payment provider converts it at their rate.

Is there a cost or a minimum?

No fee to join and no traffic minimum. The only minimum is the payout threshold, so we do not send transfers of a few euros.

Where do I see my numbers?

In your affiliate dashboard at app.getbrandgeo.com/affiliate: clicks, leads, sales, every commission with its status, and every payout with its reference. Customers are shown as masked emails, never full contact details.

Can I run several programs?

Yes. One login, one dashboard, a separate referral link per program. Approval is per program.
