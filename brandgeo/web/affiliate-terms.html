These terms govern participation in any affiliate program operated by BrandGEO Global ("we", "us") through getbrandgeo.com and app.getbrandgeo.com, including programs we run for partner brands. 1. Eligibility and approval

  1. You must be at least 18 and able to enter a binding agreement, acting as a business or a self-employed professional.
  2. Membership is per program. We approve or decline every application at our discretion and may ask for more information about how you promote.
  3. You may be a member of several programs under one account. Each program's rules (commission, attribution window, approval period, minimum payout, currency) are shown on the program card and in your dashboard, and form part of these terms.

2. Referral links, codes and attribution

  1. You receive a personal referral link per program and, where offered, a coupon code. Only links and codes issued by us count.
  2. A customer is attributed to you when they buy within the program's attribution window after clicking your link (last click by default, first click where the program says so), or when they use your coupon code. A coupon code takes precedence over a link. We may attribute a customer to you by hand when they tell us you referred them; our decision on attribution is final.
  3. Attribution uses a first-party cookie set only with the visitor's consent, and otherwise a session-only record. We do not guarantee attribution where the visitor blocks storage.
  4. Self-referrals are not paid: you may not earn on your own purchases, on companies you own or control, or on companies you invoice for BrandGEO services yourself.

3. Commission

  1. Commission is calculated by us, on our systems, from the program rules in force when the qualifying event happened and from the amount the customer actually paid us, net of VAT, discounts and payment fees. Where a program pays a fixed amount per qualified lead, we decide whether a lead qualifies.
  2. Recurring commission, where a program offers it, is paid on renewals for the number of months stated, counted from the customer's first payment, and stops when the subscription ends.
  3. A commission is pending until the program's approval period has passed, then approved. We may reject a commission that results from a breach of these terms, from fraud, or from a transaction that is disputed, refunded or unpaid.
  4. If a purchase is refunded, cancelled or charged back, the related commission is reversed. If it was already paid to you, the amount is deducted from your next payout.

4. Payouts

  1. Approved commissions are grouped into a payout per currency once they reach the program's minimum payout. We pay monthly, by Wise, Revolut, PayPal or bank transfer, to the details you keep in your dashboard. We do not convert currency; your provider does.

  3. You are responsible for invoicing where your country requires it, and for all taxes, social charges and registrations that apply to you. Your dashboard provides payout statements; we do not provide tax advice.
