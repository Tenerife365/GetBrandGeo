1. Overview

This page describes how BrandGEO ("we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on getbrandgeo.com and app.getbrandgeo.com.

2. Current Cookie Usage

getbrandgeo.com uses Google Analytics 4, and it is loaded only if you accept it. Until you choose, nothing is requested from Google and no analytics cookies are set. If you accept, Google Analytics sets _ga and _ga_<id> cookies to distinguish returning visitors. If you decline, they are never set and the Google script is never loaded at all.

We also use Plausible, which runs for every visitor. Plausible sets no cookies, stores no identifier and collects no personal data, which is why it does not ask for consent and is not covered by the banner.

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Advertising signals are held at denied in Google’s consent settings even after you accept analytics, because the banner asks about analytics only.

The dashboard (app.getbrandgeo.com) uses only essential session cookies required for authentication.

3. Essential Cookies

Essential cookies are required for the dashboard to function (for example, keeping you signed in) and cannot be disabled without affecting core functionality.

4. Analytics Cookies and Your Choice

When you first visit, a banner asks whether to load Google Analytics. Accepting and declining are presented as equally weighted choices; declining is never made harder than accepting, and there is no pre-selected option.

Your choice is stored in your browser’s local storage under bg-consent, not in a cookie, so that declining does not itself require setting one.

5. Managing Cookies and Changing Your Mind

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer of any page. Withdrawing is as easy as giving consent. You can also control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time; blocking essential cookies may prevent the dashboard from functioning correctly.

6. Contact

For any questions about this Cookie Policy, contact us at contact@getbrandgeo.com.
