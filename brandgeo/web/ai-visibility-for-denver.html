AI Visibility in Denver: Colorado's Economy, Mirrored Twice

We ran 6 real Denver categories (personal injury law, real estate, property management, immigration law, energy/natural resources law, and cannabis business law) through the same AI Visibility pipeline BrandGEO runs for paying clients, across five AI engines. The headline: Vicente LLP reached 4 of 5 engines for cannabis business law, and three separate firms converged at 3 of 5 each for energy and natural resources law, both categories mapping directly onto Colorado's real economy.

Property management again showed two firms at 4 of 5, extending its status as the most reliably high-consensus category across every city measured. Individual real estate agents, as usual, stayed completely fragmented.

Colorado's real economy, mirrored twice in one dataset

Vicente LLP, a genuine Denver-headquartered firm and one of the most nationally recognized cannabis-law practices, reached 4 of 5 engines for cannabis business law. Separately, three different energy and natural resources firms, Davis Graham & Stubbs, Womble Bond Dickinson, and Holland & Hart, all converged at 3 of 5 each. Two unrelated categories, both tied directly to what Colorado's economy actually is.

Property management keeps proving reliable

Pioneer Property Management and Real Property Management Colorado both reached 4 of 5 engines in Denver, extending a pattern that's now held across nearly every city researched: New York (3/4), Los Angeles (4/5), Chicago (5/5), Houston (4/5 twice), Dallas (3/5 three times), Miami (4/5), Philadelphia (4/5 twice), Phoenix (5 firms at 3/5), San Francisco, Seattle, and San Diego (Ziprent's West Coast pattern), and now Denver. No other category type has been this consistently high-consensus across the whole program.

A data-quality note, not a finding

All 5 engines returned usable data on every prompt this run, no collection errors. One agent-matching service was misidentified as a real estate agent and removed as noise: "Agent Pronto." This run also used Google AI Mode in place of the now-retired Meta AI engine used in earlier city pages.

What this means if your business is in Denver

  1. 1

    If you're in cannabis law, you're competing against your state's own identity

    Vicente LLP benefits from being tied to what Colorado is actually known for. A competing firm needs a specific plan to break into that kind of category-defining consensus.

  2. 2

    Energy law is winnable, not locked down

    Three firms all sit at the same 3/5 consensus level, no single dominant leader. There's real room to enter that set without unseating an entrenched incumbent.

  3. 3

    Individual real estate agents are the easiest door in

    No agent name crossed any two engines. That's the best-documented opportunity in Denver for a specific agent or team to become the AI-recommended name.

  4. 4

    Re-run this regularly, not once

    These answers reflect what the engines say today, this week. Engine behavior and extraction quality both shift between runs. A one-time check tells you less than an ongoing one.
