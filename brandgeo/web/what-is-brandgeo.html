About BrandGEO

What is BrandGEO?

A buyer asks ChatGPT for “the best accounting firm in Madrid” or “a CRM for a small agency” and gets a short list of names. BrandGEO tells you whether your business is on that list, on which engines, and what to change if it is not.

The gaps become a prioritised fix list. You can run it yourself from €0, or have the BrandGEO team run it for you.

● How it works

From a list of questions to a list of fixes

  1. You choose the questions. These are the prompts a buyer types before choosing a supplier: “best X in my city”, “X alternatives”, “which X for a small business”. Plans hold from 5 to 200 of them.
  2. BrandGEO asks the engines. Each prompt goes to every engine on your plan, from your chosen market, and every answer is stored.
  3. Each answer is read for five things: whether your brand is named, its place in a ranked list, the tone of the mention, the text around it, and which competitors were named.
  4. The results become a score and a fix list. The dashboard shows your visibility per engine and per prompt over time, and ranks what to do next as P0, P1 and P2 fixes.
  5. You fix it, or we do. Growth and Growth PRO add a site audit with fix drafts. On Managed, the BrandGEO team does the research, the audit and the monthly report for you.

● Common questions

What is BrandGEO and what does it do?

BrandGEO is an AI visibility monitoring platform. It sends the questions your buyers ask (for example “best accounting firm in Madrid” or “which CRM suits a small agency”) to up to seven AI engines, records every answer, and shows whether your brand is named, where it is placed in a ranked list, the tone of the mention, and which competitors were named instead. It then turns the gaps into a prioritised fix list.

Which AI engines does BrandGEO check?

Up to seven: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Grok, Google AI Mode and Google AI Overviews. The Free plan checks Gemini, Radar adds Claude, Essentials adds ChatGPT, Growth adds Perplexity and Google AI Mode, and Growth PRO adds Grok and Google AI Overviews.

What are the tools to monitor my brand in ChatGPT and Gemini?

There are three kinds. Dedicated AI visibility platforms: BrandGEO, Peec AI, Profound, Otterly.AI, Scrunch AI, AthenaHQ, Rankscale and Goodie AI. SEO and content platforms that added AI tracking: Semrush (AI Visibility Toolkit), Ahrefs (Brand Radar) and Conductor. Social listening tools that sell AI visibility as a separate product: Brand24, through its sister product Chatbeat. The right pick depends on budget, which engines you need, and whether you want to do the work yourself.

Why is my business not mentioned in AI chatbot answers?

Usually because the engines do not find enough clear, consistent, third-party evidence about what you do, where, and for whom. Engines lean on directories, review sites, comparison pages and press that name a business next to its category. BrandGEO shows which engines leave you out, for which questions, and which competitors they name instead, so you can see where the evidence is missing.

Is there a service that does the work for me?

Yes. BrandGEO Managed (from 1,500 euros a month) runs the work for you: prompt research, a competitor and website audit, a monthly executive report and a monthly strategy call, across all seven engines and up to 200 prompts. The software plans, from Free to Growth PRO, are for teams that want to run it themselves.

What does BrandGEO cost?

Free is 0 euros (5 prompts, Gemini, monthly refresh). Radar is 29 euros a month at the launch price for the first 100 customers, then 39 euros (7 prompts, Gemini and Claude, weekly). Essentials is 99 euros a month (18 prompts, ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude). Growth is 299 euros a month (35 prompts, 5 engines). Growth PRO is 449 euros a month (56 prompts, 7 engines). Managed is from 1,500 euros a month. Enterprise is priced on request.

Can agencies or companies with several brands use BrandGEO?

Free and Radar cover one project each. Running several brands or markets under one account is part of Custom Enterprise, which is scoped with you.
