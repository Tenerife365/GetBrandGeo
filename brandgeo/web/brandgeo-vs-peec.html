● The Honest Version

Where Peec AI genuinely wins

  • Faster self-serve onboarding if your team wants to configure everything themselves from day one.
  • Unlimited seats on every plan, useful if a lot of people across your org need dashboard access.
  • Strong existing track record, a 4.9/5 G2 rating and an established base of 2,500+ marketing teams.
  • Purpose-built agency bundles for managing several client brands under one account.

Where BrandGEO genuinely wins

  • A real managed-service tier, Peec has no equivalent at any price; the alternative is a separate $1,500 to 5,000+/month agency retainer.
  • All 5 core engines included from Growth up, with no per-engine add-on pricing to track.
  • One platform across the whole journey, start self-serve on Free or Essentials, move to fully managed on Managed or Pro, without switching vendors.
  • A free one-time audit with no signup required, if you just want a baseline first.

Is BrandGEO cheaper than Peec AI?

At the entry tier, Peec Starter ($95/mo) and BrandGEO Essentials (€99/mo) land close together. The real gap is at the done-for-you end: BrandGEO Managed starts at €900/month for a fully managed service, versus the $1,500 to 5,000+/month floor of a dedicated GEO agency retainer, which is the actual alternative once you want the work done for you, since Peec doesn't offer that option at any price.

Does Peec AI offer a managed service like BrandGEO?

No. Peec AI is self-serve software across every tier, including Enterprise. You or your team configure prompts, review data, and act on it yourselves. BrandGEO offers that same self-serve path on its lower tiers, plus a genuinely managed option on Managed, Pro, and Enterprise.

Which AI engines does each platform track?

Peec's Starter and Pro tiers let you choose 3 models from ChatGPT, AI Mode, AI Overviews, Microsoft Copilot, Perplexity, and Gemini; Advanced allows more; Enterprise unlocks all models including Claude and open models via API. BrandGEO's Growth tier and above include all 5 core engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and Google AI Mode) as standard.
