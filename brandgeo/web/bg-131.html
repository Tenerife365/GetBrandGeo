AI Visibility in Nashville: Engines Agree on Music Lawyers, Not on Real Estate Agents

We asked ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini six local buyer questions about Nashville. Between them they named 110 firms, and 9 were on all three lists: 3 of them music law firms, none of them a real estate agent.

Ask three AI engines the same question about Nashville and you get three lists. We wanted to know how much those lists overlap, because a business that appears on only one of them is invisible to buyers who use the other two.

On November 6, 2026 we put six local buyer questions about Nashville to ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini: a personal injury law firm, real estate agents, a property management company, an immigration lawyer, and two questions written for this city, music industry and entertainment lawyers and healthcare law firms. Then we counted the firms each engine named and the ones all three had in common.

How this was measured, and what it cannot tell you

18 answers: six questions, three engines (ChatGPT on gpt-5.5, Claude on claude-sonnet-4-6, Gemini on gemini-2.5-flash), all collected on November 6, 2026. We took every name an engine put in bold, then read each of the three answers to the same question to see whether it named that firm anywhere, in bold or in running text. Ratings, directory names and advice were removed. Firms named after a person are reported as a count, not by name, and that covers nearly every law firm here.

Claude's stored answers stop at about 3,000 characters and five of its six were cut mid-list, so its counts are a lower bound. This is one day of answers. It does not rank anyone and it does not say why an engine chose a firm.

Of the 110 firms, 9 were on all three lists (8%), 26 on two (24%) and 75 on one engine only (68%). Eight of the nine shared names are law firms. The ninth is a property manager.

Music and entertainment law: three firms on every list

This is the question written for Nashville, and it produced the most agreement: three firms were named in all three answers. All three are large firms with a Nashville office, and all carry founders' names, so we count them and do not print them. Two more firms were named by two engines and 12 by one only.

The engines also answered in different units. ChatGPT listed 8 firms and put notable lawyers under each. Claude led with individual lawyers and attached a firm to some of them. Gemini gave two lists, one of people and one of firms. Three individual lawyers were named in all three answers, which is as many as the firms. A firm that checks only for its own name would miss how often an engine recommends the person instead.

Real estate agents: 21 names, none in common

ChatGPT named 7 agents or teams, Claude 7 agents in 6 teams, Gemini 11. Not one agent or team was in all three answers. Three were named by two engines, among them Oak Street Real Estate Group, which ChatGPT listed as a team and Claude listed twice through two of its agents. The other 18 were named once.

One local brokerage did appear in all three answers, but only as the affiliation of three different agents. The engines agreed on where good agents work more than on who they are.

All three engines named Evernest, one of the national brands we counted in BG-113. Six more companies were named by two engines: HomeRiver Group, Mynd, Prime Rentals & Real Estate and Gemstone Solutions by Claude and Gemini, plus two firms named after people. Eighteen companies were named by one engine only, 10 of them by ChatGPT, which was the only engine to add commercial property managers to its list.

Personal injury, immigration and healthcare law

Personal injury: 16 firms, 2 on all three lists and 5 on two. Immigration: 13 firms, 2 on all three and 4 on two. ChatGPT named only 5 immigration firms and gave the rest of its answer to nonprofit help and questions to ask; Claude and Gemini named 8 each.

Healthcare law: 18 firms, 1 on all three lists and 6 on two. Five of those six were shared by ChatGPT and Claude, whose lists were close to each other, while Gemini's list was mostly its own: 6 of its 8 firms appeared in no other answer. One of them is a firm that ChatGPT's answer to the same question describes as absorbed into another firm on its list. We did not check which is current. A buyer who read only one answer would not know there was anything to check.

An exact match on bold text finds 3 firms on all three lists; reading the answers finds 9. The six it missed were all written differently from one answer to the next: a short name in one and the long legal name in another, a lawyer's name in bold with the firm in brackets after it, a firm attached to a person with a dash. For a business owner checking their own visibility, search the full answer for your name and its variants, not only the highlighted list.

What a Nashville business should do

Ask each engine your own buyer question, by name of your trade and "Nashville", and note which list you are on. Treat the three as separate channels: 101 of the 110 firms named here were missing from at least one engine, and 75 were on one list only.

If you are an agent or a lawyer, search for your own name as well as the firm's. The engines recommended people in some answers and firms in others, and in real estate they agreed on neither.

Pick one form of your firm's name and use it everywhere: your site, your Google Business Profile, your directory listings. Six of the nine firms all three engines named were written in different forms across the answers. Read the answers for what they say about you too, not only whether you are in them.
